Ages of Earth

Lead your people from the Stone Age to the age of gunpowder.

How to play
  • Goal: destroy every building and unit of your rival before they do it to you.
  • Select with a left click, or drag a box around units. Shift adds to the selection; double-click picks every unit of that kind on screen.
  • Command with a right click: on the ground to move, on an enemy to attack, on trees, berries, stone or gold to gather, on your building site to help build, on a farm to work it.
  • Citizens gather food, wood, stone and gold and carry it to your Town Center or a Storehouse. Press B with citizens selected to build.
  • Houses make room for 5 more people. Farms never run out once the berries do.
  • The triangle: infantry beat cavalry, archers beat infantry, cavalry beat archers. Mix your army.
  • Epochs: at the Town Center, advance from the Stone Age to the Bronze Age, the Middle Ages and the Gunpowder Age. Every unit re-arms and every building is rebuilt in the new style.
  • Camera: arrow keys, the screen edges, middle-drag or Space+drag to pan; the wheel zooms; [ and ] turn the view and Backspace straightens it. Click the minimap to jump.
  • Keys: . next idle citizen, H Town Center, Ctrl+1–9 make a group and 1–9 recall it, A attack-move, Del delete, P pause, Alt shows all health bars, F10 menu.

Best on a laptop or desktop with a mouse. All sound is made live in your browser.
