# QR code of the save key (follow-up to #290, #292)

- **What:** Cloud saves panel → "Show QR code" draws a QR of `<site>/#vault=KEY` (`src/ui/qr.ts`, dependency `qrcode-generator`). Scanning on a phone opens the game, takes the key out of the address bar at once, and asks before switching to it, then opens Cloud saves.
- **Why the fragment:** a `#` part is never sent to a server or in a Referer, so the key isn't logged.
- **Seen:** headless Chromium (vite dev): `#vault=ABCD-…` was stripped from the URL, the key was adopted, the QR drew, no page errors. **Not seen:** a real phone camera scanning it; the QR's decode was not checked by a reader.
