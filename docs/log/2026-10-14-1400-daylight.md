# The hours of the day (#97), 2026-10-14

Own pitch, lane look. `src/render/daylight.ts` gives the light for an hour; `View.light` applies it (relit only when the hour moves) under the weather's mood. One day is `DAY_SECONDS` (3600) of play; the opening hour (07:30–10:30) comes from the seed. `?hour=` pins the clock. No rules, save or data.ts change.

Tried: headless Chromium, title screen, `?hour=` 7, 12, 17.5, 19.2, 21, 2: no page or console errors; mean ground colour fell from about green 98 at noon to 61–66 at night, with long warm shadows at dusk. Not tried: a match in play, the HUD at night, low quality. Sound: none added.
