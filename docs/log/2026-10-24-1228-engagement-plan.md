# The engagement plan, "distance is difference", and continue-or-spawn

Planned with the owner in chat: `docs/engagement.md`. Phase 1 shows the road both ways (a road planner with a pinned goal and step-backs, a card that reads a rival's age changes and step-back windows, an end screen that draws each journey with a Rematch). Phase 2 builds up the town: every side age a building of its own as its keepsake, then main-road ages that change the town, then (research) a layout rule for each people. Then rivals with a temperament and handicaps for friends; then a seed of the week and a codex. Dilemma cards and the campaign are out; a mid-game richness question is research only.

`scripts/unlocks.ts` counts what each age opens: the Middle Ages and Gunpowder open no building and no new kind of unit; 7 of ~25 side ages have a building of their own; 7 of 14 keepsakes are shared buildings.

The owner's idea, recorded in `docs/rulings.md` and under lever 1 of `docs/verbs.md`: distance is difference, so far enough out on a road is a whole new strategy. Also the successor rule: tokens decide whether to continue or spawn, and owner-started sessions spawn too (`docs/successors.md`).

Unseen: docs and a script only; no game change.
Next: road planner (step 1) and side-age buildings (step A) on two chains.
