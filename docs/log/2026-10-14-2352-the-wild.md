# The wild: mammoths and wolf packs (#171, lane rules)

The owner, in chat: the Stone Age "feels mechanical … like designated steps … a mechanical race". So the early map now pushes back: a mammoth per town (`MAMMOTH.home` 17 tiles out) that charges its hunters and leaves 600 food, more in the open, and wolf dens (`WOLF.dens` per map size, at least `WOLF.home` 22 tiles from every town, by wild gold where there is some) whose packs attack anyone within `WOLF.notice` and chase no further than `WOLF.leash` from the den.

- Rules: `src/sim/animals.ts` (beasts fight; `fightBack` queues the interrupted work), `huntStep` in `units.ts` (soldiers hunt; a wolf leaves no carcass), the `hunt` command takes soldiers, `placeWild` in `mapgen.ts` (own dice, touches no tile; skirmish/online only, not campaign maps).
- Computer: `wilds()` in `ai.ts`, numbers in `AI_WILD`; deer hunting now ignores beasts.
- Look and sound: `mammoth`/`wolf` models in `unitModels.ts`, batches per kind in `unitsView.ts`, `mammoth`/`howl`/`snarl` sounds, bigger mammoth carcass, minimap colours, two tips, a wild-specific attack warning.
- Tests: `tests/wild.test.ts` (9). `npm run check` 320 pass; `browser-smoke` passes.
- Save: no bump. New `Animal` fields are optional; old saves hold only deer.
- `scripts/wild-watch.ts` plays computer matches and reports beasts felled and people lost to them.
