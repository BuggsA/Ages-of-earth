# The Water Road, s4: the Age of the Water Line (#701)

Chain 1024-2000, session 4 of 8, lane rules (hard).

- **The age:** `waterline`, tier 3, instead of the Gunpowder Age, route `water`, keepsake the Sluice, no walls or gates, `sluices: true` (woven into a Golden Age). Roads: dykes → waterline (the trunk, `walked: 'dykes'`), gunpowder → waterline (a turn for a people that walked the Dykes), waterline → gunpowder (cheap) and → industrial (the trunk's price).
- **The Sluice:** 1 tile, placed as a dyke is (shallows, a channel, the shore), or into its owner's own finished dyke line in place of a piece. It is stamped as a dyke (its people walk across it). It holds the sea back for `floodFront` from the moment it is laid out, built or not, open or shut (`banked`). It trains gun-barges. Opening and shutting reuses the `pack` order at a Sluice (no new command: `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` pins the list).
- **The flood:** `sluiceStep`, every `INUND.every` s. From each open, working, fed sluice (still water four-ways beside it), `waterLine` reaches four ways over low ground up to `INUND.reach` steps. Low ground is polder, or open grass or sand whose top corner is at most `waterTop` (the sluice's lowest land bank + `INUND.rise`). One ring rises a step; flooded ground no sluice reaches ebbs a ring from its edges in. A flooded tile is `T_WATER` with no `B_TERRAIN`, `s.inund[t] = terrain + 1`, and its heights are untouched. It is kept out of the mills' water (`wet`, `pull`) and the sea's (`runs`), so it is never pumped and never counts as open sea. `flood` and `drain` clear it.
- **Units:** land units wade at `INUND.wade`, siege at `INUND.siege` (`wading(s, x, y, siege)`). Farms mostly under water yield 0 (`drowned`, `fieldBoost`). Boats keep a tile wet while water lies beside it; a boat on a tile that ebbs is lost.
- **Gun-barge:** a new `barge` unit (ship class, water domain, tiers 3–4). It has its own pass, `BARGE` (grid.ts, path.ts, `passOf`): open water, wadeable water (channels, the flood), and any finished sluice on water (`lock`).
- **The computer:** `wants('waterline')` for a people that walked the Dykes, on its own coin (salt 0x5a1c, `AI_WATERLINE.take`). It stays `AI_WATERLINE.stay` s. `waterLineWork`: one sluice at `sluiceSpot` (the most flooded ground within `reach`, needing at least 8 tiles), two barges, open while `threat`+ enemy land soldiers stand within `near`, shut `calm` s after. Idle barges attack the nearest. Barges count with its navy. `dykeWork` counts a sluice in its line as a dyke.
- **Look and sound:** a sluice model (piers, beam, winch, and a leaf that is wound up when open, with white water). A barge model (a pram with a bow gun; a steam gunboat with a funnel). Terrain paints drowned meadow, with a translucent sheet of water `FLOOD_DEPTH` over flooded tiles (`floodSheet`). Barges ride the sheet. Sounds: `sluiceOpen`, `sluiceShut`, `spread`. The minimap and terrain repaint on `s.inundated`. A toast when a rival opens a sluice you can see. The HUD has an Open/Shut button (O), a status line, and a farmer's "waiting for the water" line. Two tips. Web map: lane 5, row 3.5.
- **Save:** no bump. The new state is optional (`inund`, `inundated`, `Building.open`, `AiMemory.line`), absent in a v5 save.
- **Numbers new in data.ts:** `INUND = { every: 1, reach: 7, rise: 0.4, wade: 0.3, siege: 0.1 }`, `AI_WATERLINE = { take: 0.6, reach: 16, barges: 2, threat: 4, near: 14, calm: 20, stay: 360 }`, the Sluice (hp 800, 60 wood 80 stone, 20 s), the Gun-barge (200 hp, 24 attack, range 8, 130 wood 70 gold) and Steam Gunboat, and road prices (dykes → waterline 900f 500w 300g 300s, 60 s; gunpowder → waterline 400f 300w 150s, 40 s; waterline → gunpowder 200f 150g, 25 s).
