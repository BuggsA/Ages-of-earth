# Route sweeps (#555 s0, first slice)

`scripts/routes.ts` (harness `src/sim/routePlay.ts`, test `tests/route-play.test.ts`): a Hard computer in seat 0 is bound to a route (every side age made up: the route's ages yes, all others no; the blood fork left to its own choice) against a Normal computer that walks the web freely. Navy runs on `lakes` and `sea`, the rest on `continental`. `npx tsx scripts/routes.ts --seeds 6 -j 4` (about 5 min). Small maps, 40 min cap.

## Reading it

- **Each route wins at about the plain road's rate** (3–6 of 6). None is a trap on the maps it names. Horse is lowest (3/6); worth more seeds.
- **The computer does not play a route start to finish.** Even bound to it, most matches end (a win, or a loss) before the second or later route age, and the Army route reaches only the Warband in 6 seeds, never Iron and Blood. The first-seed loss stays at the Bronze Age (a rush). So "the computer must be able to play each route" is not yet met: the matches end first, or the road's deeds are not done in time.
- Wins are with a few ages walked, so the sweeps show the routes aren't weak, not that their late ages are strong.
