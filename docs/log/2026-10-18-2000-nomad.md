# Born on the move (#501, the Mongols)

- `isNomad` (`src/sim/nomad.ts`, `CIVS.mongols.nomad`, `NOMAD` in data.ts): `isMobile` is true in any age, so the Town Center and Houses pack onto wagons (J) and pitch (P) from the Stone Age; `createGame` gives each Town Center `NOMAD.start` (4) sheep. Farms, walls and gates are barred (`buildBarred`); citizens refuse stone and bog iron (`mineable`, `nomadSpurns`); gold is still dug (first draft barred it too, and the computer then could not buy stone or climb: wood piled to 4000 unsold, `ai-ore-sweep` failed). No new command or state, so no save bump; older Mongol replays differ.
- The flock is the farm: per Mongol ewe `NOMAD.milk` (4) a bite, grass back in `NOMAD.regrow` (20) s, citizens milk `NOMAD.milkSpeed` (1.8) times as fast, `NOMAD.cap` (12) sheep a Town Center (a second test, `ai-steppe` "slaughters a sheep", fixes this at 12 with the 4 started).
- Tribute: civ `add.tribute` 0.2 (stacks with the Horde's 0.3); riders also take `NOMAD.stone` (0.5) of the gold due in stone from the rival's stores (`tributeStep`; event `tribute` gains `stone`). No tribute on the Silk Road (`exacting`). Look: horsetail standard at the Town Center, grey chips with stone tribute; HUD gold/stone hints, tip `nomad`; the farm tip is off for Mongols; README, design notes, goals.
- Computer: `nomadLambs` (lambs before citizens up to `aiLambs` + citizens/`aiPerSheep`), milkers per `aiPerMilker`, a Market from 12 citizens, sells food/wood over 450 while gold is under 800, buys stone under 80, a second camp (`aiCamps`) with the starting stone, no stone miners (`wanted`, `sendTo`), no iron road. It still raises the Hunting Camp that opens the Steppe road in the Stone Age. Riders are held back for a tribute band only from 6 riders (so the merchant ambush in `ai-web` works).
- A/B (`civ-ab.ts`, `nomad` added to the `off` list, which also drops the tribute): Mongols on Hard vs Greeks on Hard, seeds 1-6 x 2 seats, 25 min medium. Loop off (old play): won 4, lost 2, ahead 4, behind 3. Loop on, final numbers: won 5, lost 1, ahead 1, behind 5. At least parity in decisive matches, but twice as many matches end behind (slower ages): the nomad economy is leaner. Only against Greeks; not against Egyptians or Romans. Early versions (one camp, no milker speed) won 0 of 12.
- Numbers to find when playing: `NOMAD`, civ `add.tribute` in data.ts.
- Not seen in a browser (standard, tips, effects).
