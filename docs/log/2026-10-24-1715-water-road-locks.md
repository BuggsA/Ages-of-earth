# The Water Road, s5: the Age of Locks (#701)

Chain 1024-2000, session 5 of 8, lane rules (hard).

- **The age:** `locks`, tier 4, instead of the Industrial Age, route `water`, keepsake the Dam, no walls or gates, `canals` and `turbines` (both woven into a Golden Age, should one ever follow it). Roads: `industrial → locks` (a turn for a people that walked the Water Line), `locks → industrial` (cheap), `locks → atomic` (the trunk's price). The trunk road `waterline → locks` is **not** in: `tests/waterline.test.ts:79` pins the Water Line's roads, so it waits on the owner ([#701 comment](https://github.com/BuggsA/Ages-of-earth/issues/701#issuecomment-6387177804), `docs/status.md`).
- **Canals:** a Channel whose ditch fills while its people stands in the age is a canal: `GameState.channel` 2, `T_WATER` with no `B_TERRAIN`, corners sunk to `CANAL.bank`/`bed`. Land units swim it at `CANAL.wade`; every ship sails it (`sails` in `grid.ts`, read by `walkable` and the path fields). A Channel laid on an old channel in the age digs it deep. The HUD calls the button "Canal" in the age.
- **Bridge:** 1 tile, on a channel or canal (`bridgeSpot`), stamped as a gate (its own team walks it at full pace, `wading` 1), and ships and barges pass under it (`underBridge`, `lock`).
- **Dam:** 1 tile, placed as a sluice is. Stamped as a dyke, holds the sea (`banked`), stops ships. Turning (`damTurns`: finished, in the age or kept, awake, still water beside it), its owner's buildings within `DAM.reach` work at `DAM.drive` (`workRate` × the coils' rate). Falling with water beside it, it **bursts** (`burst`, from `removeDead`): `waterLine` to `DAM.surge` inundated at once, land units there lose `DAM.hurt`, then it drains as any flood. Kegs hit it `BREACH.keg` times; it takes wall damage from soldiers.
- **The computer:** `wants('locks')` on its own salt (0x10c5, `AI_LOCKS.take`); `turnRoad` takes `industrial → locks`. `riverWork` digs its channel line deep in the age; `locksWork` raises a Dam at `damSpot` (most training buildings within reach, not on its canal) and a Bridge on the canal tile nearest home. Stays `AI_LOCKS.stay` s.
- **Look and sound:** bridge model (piers, pivot drum, deck, railings, a lamp in the owner's colour), dam model (battered wall, brick turbine house, spillway with white water while turning); a canal's bed of cut stone and a towpath on its banks (the terrain also repaints a channel dug deep); the burst's spray and `damBurst` sound; a toast to the owner and to a rival who sees it. HUD lines for dam and bridge, two tips, web map lane 5 row 4.5, dev hook `ages.locks(0)`.
- **Save:** no bump (new state is optional: `channel` 2, `AiMemory.locks`).
- **Numbers new in data.ts:** `CANAL = { bank: -0.3, bed: -0.75, wade: 0.25 }`, `DAM = { reach: 8, drive: 140, surge: 6, hurt: 40 }`, `AI_LOCKS = { take: 0.6, reach: 14, stay: 360 }`, the Bridge (500 hp, 50 wood 30 stone, 12 s, key A), the Dam (1200 hp, 100 wood 150 stone, 30 s, key N), road prices (`industrial → locks` 500f 400w 250s, 45 s; `locks → industrial` 250f 200g, 30 s).
- **Tests:** `tests/locks.test.ts` (9), `tests/ai-locks.test.ts` (1), `tests/slow/ai-locks-road.test.ts` (1, about 20 s).
- **Browser:** `npx vite`, lakes map, `ages.locks(0)`: a 19 to 21-tile canal from the shore past the Town Center, the dam and bridge on it, a warship on it. The warship sailed up the canal and stopped at the dam. The dam's panel read "⚙ Turning: drives 1 building that trains".
