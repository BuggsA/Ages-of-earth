# The road planner (#725, s1 of #724)

`routeTo(s, p, goal)` in `src/sim/ages.ts`: Dijkstra over ages on roads `roadKnown` allows plus step-back edges (trunk age one tier down; a second step back in one route pays the full cooldown). Deterministic (ties to the earlier road in `ROADS`). Panel in `src/ui/roadPlanner.ts` (Roads button, open state and goal in localStorage under `ages-planner`); the web map's cards carry a 📍 that pins the goal.

Limits, on purpose: only the first leg shows keep/sleep/wake/lose lines (`planLines` reads the age you stand in); roads that open only once a window is walked mid-journey appear when you get there and the plan is made again. No numbers in `data.ts` changed; no saves touched.

Decision added for the player: I plan a journey (push on, detour back for a crossing) with its price in sight.

Seen in a browser (headless Chromium): panel opens, goal select plans Stone to Gunpowder in 3 steps, open state survives a reload. Not seen: step-back and crossing routes in play (covered by `tests/road-planner.test.ts`), the web-map pin, phone width.
