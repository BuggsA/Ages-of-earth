# The Wild Hunt gets a building of its own: the Falconer's Mews (#727)

Chain 1025-1752, session 8. #859 (Chantry) was still in ci when I began; built this on its branch.

The **Falconer's Mews** (`mews`, key U in the military menu, Age of the Wild Hunt only, 70 wood 30 stone, 600 hp, 2 tiles, sees 9) trains War Wolves and Hawks. `barred` skips the tamed-kind check at a mews and `trainsAt` adds the hawk, so the pack is bred at the front with no rider and no tamed wolf. The computer (clever) raises one `AI_MEWS.out` (20) tiles toward the nearest rival Town Center and breeds wolves there up to `AI_MEWS.wolves` (6) (`raiseMews`, `ai.ts`); the general training loop skips it.

**Decision:** reinforce the pack at a forward mews (razable, far from home) or keep breeding riders and wolves at the Stables at home.
**Verbs:** verb: breed wolves and fly hawks at the front; door: the Wild Hunt only, sleeps on leaving the age; two players: forward mews vs home Stables; answer: raid the mews.
