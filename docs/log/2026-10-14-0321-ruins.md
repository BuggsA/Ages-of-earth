# Ancient ruins (#64)

Session `claude/upbeat-cori-rc8bef`, a pitch of my own. Lane rules, plus a small separate function in `src/sim/ai.ts`.

- **Rules:** `src/sim/ruins.ts` (finds, lost-knowledge choice, the dig), `Ruin` and `RuinFind` in `types.ts`, the `dig` order and command, `RUINS` in `data.ts`, `placeRuins` in `mapgen.ts` (own dice, after everything else, only when `deposits`, so campaign maps are unchanged), `ruinOnTile` in `grid.ts` (no building on or beside a ruin). `restat` moved unchanged from `game.ts` to `world.ts` so the lore find can use it.
- **Computer:** `excavate` in `ai.ts`: two citizens to the nearest ruin with no rival soldiers in sight within 12 tiles and no known rival Town Center or tower within 20, once it has 10 citizens and no threat at home; it flees if soldiers come within 9 and keeps off that ruin for 3 minutes. Headless matches (`scripts/sim.ts` 3, 5 and 8, medium) dug every ruin.
- **Look and sound:** `src/render/ruinsView.ts` (a standing colonnade, a dug pit; the viewer sees each as last seen), a dust-and-shimmer effect, a `ruinFind` cue, a minimap mark, a tip, a HUD panel, and a banner for the finder.
- **Found on the way:** `Music.style()` recursed forever outside side ages (#55), so music threw a stack overflow every bar on `main`. Fixed to fall back to `STYLES[this.epoch]`, as it did before #55.
- **Tried in headless Chromium:** started a match, left-clicked a ruin (panel shows), box-selected three citizens and right-clicked it (three dig orders), ran it on: the pit and the relic appear. No page errors after the music fix. Not heard: the sound cue and music are unverified by ear.
