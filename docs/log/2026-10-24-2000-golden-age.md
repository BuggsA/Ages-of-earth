# The Golden Age (#703)

The owner asked for an age that "feels almost too good" and makes players wonder what ages are out there, since many side ages felt hard to reach or not worth it.

- New age `golden` (tier 3, turn off the Gunpowder Age). Road needs 3 side ages walked (`lives: { seen: 2, n: 3 }`), costs food 900, wood 400, gold 900, stone 300, 60 s, `gate: 'hard'`.
- While in it, `ageDef` weaves every walked side age (verbs, best gifts, no doors); their buildings wake. Own gift: build speed ×1.2.
- `GOLDEN = { span: 360, more: 60, most: 600 }` seconds. Ends on its own back to the Gunpowder Age; never opens twice. Climbing to Industrial ends it early.
- Every player is told (event `golden`, banner, sound, tips); the town is gilded.
- The computer takes the road when it opens and rides the span out.
- Not yet: the computer planning a third life on purpose; rivals turtling through the window.
