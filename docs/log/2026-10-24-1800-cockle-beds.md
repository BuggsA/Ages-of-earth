# Cockle beds, and work on the flats that reads the tide (#767, s6d of #701)

Chain 1024-1330, session 4 of 8, lane rules, area:tides (hard). Follows #755 (routes that read the tide, PR #768).

- **Beds:** `layCockles` in `src/sim/tides.ts`, called once by `wakeTide`. Each tile of tidal ground of level `COCKLE.deep` (2) or lower that `hash2(x, y, seed + 767)` puts under `COCKLE.chance` (0.006) gets a patch: itself and its four neighbours as deep, each `R_COCKLE` (new, `types.ts`) with `COCKLE.amount` (60). No `B_RES`: beds don't block the walk. `GameState.cockles` marks bed tiles for regrowth. 17–65 beds a medium lakes map (seeds 1–6).
- **The tide over them:** `free()` now lets a bed tile bare and cover like any flat. Each high water, `regrowCockles` adds `COCKLE.regrow` (25), up to 60, to a bed open to the sea (`bedOpen`: no site or causeway, not in a pond); event `cockles`.
- **Picking:** `RES_OF_TILE`/`ACT_OF_TILE`/`RATE_OF_TILE` in `units.ts` (food, forage, `COCKLE.rate` 1.0). The order rides `cockle`, so a picker whose bed runs out looks for the next bed first.
- **Work that reads the tide:** `tideWorkHold(s, u, o)` at the top of `gatherStep` and `buildStep`. `Unit.ashore` holds a worker off until a tick. A bed under water sends its picker to dry ground by it until `baresAt` the bed's level. A worker standing on the flats (or a picker walking home on them), every `TIDE_WORK.check` ticks, runs `fleeFlood`: when `wetAt` its tile (the level's cover, or the next turn in a pond) comes before it could walk to `shoreFor`'s dry tile plus `TIDE_WORK.margin`, it goes there; a picker with a basket then goes on home. `idleAshore` does the same for an idle citizen left out on the flats (`think`, case idle). `setOrder` clears `ashore`. A causeway finished into a new pond re-reads walking routes (`raiseCauseways`).
- **Computer:** `cockleWork` in `ai.ts` (from `tideWork`, in the age): every `AI_COCKLE.every` s, sends up to `hands` (3) wood/berry gatherers to the nearest bed within `reach` (26) of home that stays bare `least` (25) s more; a picker waiting ashore over `idle` (25) s goes back to the trees. `tideWork`'s move-off-the-flats now skips gatherers and builders (the rule moves them and keeps their order).
- **HUD/look:** `cockleBed` model (wet patch, a pool, ripples, shells; drawn only while bared, shrinking as picked), minimap colour while bared, bed panel (🐚, "bared for Ns" / "bares in Ns" / ponded), status lines, 🌊 Ashore chip, tip `cockles`, toast on `tideAshore` (once a minute), sand splash in `view.ts`. Dev hook `ages.cockles(0)`.
- **Measured:** `npx tsx scripts/cockle-probe.ts 6 12` (new; three hard Tide computers, lakes, medium, 12 min): workers swept 0 over six seeds (main: 4 sweeps, 3 of them workers). 4 sweeps left: 3 soldiers on seed 4 waiting on the shore in a crowd that spilled onto the flats, 1 explorer. Computer pickers took 0–151 food a match.
