# The Age of the Mycelium and the Glowcap hollow (#800, Wild route step 8a)

- **Built:**
  - **The Glowcap hollow:** a fifth rare ore, `glowcap` (`RARE.glowcap`, `R_GLOWCAP`), one 4-tile hollow on about 40% of seeds. It rolls its own dice like the other veins. It has no find (the finds are a fixed six, `tests/models.test.ts`), the lodge reports it, a cave may hold it, and posts sell it where the world has none (`ORE_POST.ores`).
  - **The vein door counts a post that sells the ore** (`veinSeen`, `roadProblem`, `lacking`), so the rare age is a dearer road there, not a shut one. Saltpetre is still sold nowhere, so Alchemy is unchanged.
  - **The Age of the Mycelium** (tier 2, gateway, `routes: ['wild']`): `wild → mycelium` (vein door, 40 glowcaps), `→ gunpowder`, `→ middle`, `→ bloom` (deed: `webbed` 3,000; it shuns Leviathans).
  - **`src/sim/mycelium.ts`:** the web (`Building.web`; `nearestDrop` takes every stock; `webDrop` banks a load at once in `dropOff`); spores (`Unit.cloak`; `canHit`, `nearestEnemyUnit`, `threatNear`, the view and the minimap skip it); rot (`hurt`, wood 0.4% a second, stone 0.2%); fire burns the web back, and a torch may be set to a rival's bare web (`fire.ts`).
  - The door: soldiers cost ×1.25 (`price`).
  - A Bloom people may plant a Heart Tree (`buildBarred`), and its woods spread as a Bloom people's do (`wildwood.ts`). Ents still need the Wildwood.
  - Look and sound: the web's threads and glowcaps (`src/render/webView.ts`), the hollow (`glowcapVein`), three sounds, three effects, toasts, the HUD's "Hidden" line, two tips, the README, `docs/wild-road.md` §R "As built", `docs/age-roads.md`, and the design notes.
- **Computer:**
  - It decides on its own coin (`MYCELIUM.aiTake`), then raises a lodge in the Wild (`lodgeDue`).
  - It digs (`glowRoad`) or buys (`buyOre` now buys for vein doors too). It doesn't try to buy without a Market.
  - It holds the Wild with the price set aside (`aiHold`) and stays `aiStay` in the age.
  - It goes on to the Bloom once the deed is done and a quarter of the price is in store (`aiBanked`), and plants its Heart Tree there.
  - Every computer burns back a rival web it stands on (`torchGrowth`).
- **Numbers (data.ts):** `RARE.glowcap`, `ORE_TRADE.gold.glowcap` 70, `MYCELIUM` (all new), the four new roads; `ORE_POST.ores` gained `glowcap`.

  - The checksum also folds in `oreSold` for every rare ore, and there are four now.
