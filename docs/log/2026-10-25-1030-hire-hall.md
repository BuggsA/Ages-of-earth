# The Age of Merchants gets a building of its own: the Hire Hall (#727, step 3)

Chain 1025-0910, session 1. The Merchants had no keepsake. They now have the **Hire Hall** (`hirehall`, key V, Merchants only): it signs infantry, ranged and cavalry for gold alone at `HIREHALL.dear` (1.6) times the Market's hire price, and a sword signed there is paid once and draws **no wages** (`bringOut` flags only `isHire` swords, and the Hall is not a hiring building, so `merc` stays unset). It is the age's keepsake (`AGES.merchant.keepsake`): kept, it keeps signing swords at that price on any road (`trainCostFor`). The computer raises one by its Town Center once it has a Market (`raiseHireHall`, `ai.ts`); its generic training loop then uses it. Model `hirehall` in `buildingModels.ts` (the age's ochre palette comes through `st`); tip `hirehall`; README line. Tests `tests/hirehall.test.ts`, `tests/ai-hirehall.test.ts`.

**Decision it gives the player:** the Market's cheap sword that must be paid every 15 seconds, or the Hall's dear one that is yours; and the only way to field riders in an age with no Stable without paying a payroll.
