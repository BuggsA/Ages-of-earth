# The Water Road, s7: the Age of Floating Cities (#770, #701)

Chain 1024-2000, session 6 of 8, lane rules (hard).

- **The age:** `floating`, the trunk's seventh and last, route `water`, keepsake the Pontoon, `floats`. Its tier is 5 (the Atomic's numbers); it lies a row past the Atomic on the web map (lane 5, row 7). Reason (also on #770): growing `EPOCHS`/`TRUNK` would move `LAST_EPOCH`, read by every climb, unit form, technology and the campaign, and `tests/ages.test.ts` and `tests/web-map.test.ts` pin the trunk at six. No House and no Missile Silo (`noBuild`).
- **Roads:** `tides → floating` (1800f 1600w 1000g 300s, 100 s, coast door), `floating → tides` (300f 300g, 30 s), `floating → atomic` (500f 400g 200s, 40 s). No other way in.
- **Pontoon:** `BUILDINGS.pontoon`, 3×3, 1500 hp, 150 wood 40 gold, 30 s, room 10, garrison 10, drop for all four goods (fishing boats too, `nearestDrop`), trains citizens and fishing boats, eco key E. Only on tiles every ship sails (`pontoonSpot`), and only with a shore or the owner's deck beside it (`adrift`, a new `PlaceProblem`). Stamped as a gate like a dyke (`stampBuilding`): its owner's team walks it, ships don't pass. People step out onto its deck (`exitSpot` → `deckSpot`).
- **Linking:** `districtOf` (pontoons touching side to side, in id order); `FLOAT.city` (3) or more raise citizens (`linkProblem`, checked at the train order and when the citizen is due).
- **Towing:** the `pack` order at a Pontoon casts it off (`castOff`): a `tow` unit (water, ship class, 600 hp, speed 1.1, capacity 10) with the garrison and then its own people on the deck aboard; the rest swim (`FLOAT.swim` 4 tiles, `FLOAT.hurt` 25) or drown. `castProblem`: in the age or the keepsake, finished, no queue, open water beside it. The `pitch` order moors it within `FLOAT.spot` tiles (`pitchSpot` lets `adrift` pass); it raises itself in `STEPPE.pitch` s with its people sheltering, then they step out (`raiseCamps`).
- **Sinking:** ships do `FLOAT.hull` (3×) damage to a pontoon (`damageOf`); `sink` (from `removeDead`) makes those on its deck swim or drown.
- **The computer:** `wants('floating')` (coin salt 0xf10a, `AI_FLOAT.take` 0.5, `.onward` 0.8 after Locks or the Water Line); `floatWork` keeps the Tides `AI_FLOAT.after` (480 s) first, then saves and turns; in the age it moors pontoons with `mooringSpot` (beside its own first), at least `most` (4) and more when short of room (the House logic skips a barred House), trains fishing boats (with no Dock) and citizens at the linked town, tows its outermost district to the nearest shoal, and tows a district out of a rival's warhead ring (`ringsFor`), cancelling its queue first. It stays `stay` (420 s). Tows are kept out of the army's list.
- **Look and sound:** `src/render/floatModels.ts` (pontoon: tarred floats, plank deck, reed edges, two reed houses, a lantern mast; tow: a deck and house behind a steam tug), drawn at the water line; land units on a deck are drawn on it. Sounds `castOff`, `moor`, `founder`; spray effects; toasts to the owner (and a rival who sees a sinking).
- **HUD:** Cast off (O) on a Pontoon, Moor (P) on a tow, the district's status line, two tips. A hotkey shared by two buttons now runs the open one (`hotkey`), so the Pontoon's E works where the House's is shut.
- **Save:** no `v` bump (new optional fields only: `AiMemory.float`, unit type `tow`).
- **Numbers new in data.ts:** `FLOAT = { city: 3, hold: 10, swim: 4, hurt: 25, spot: 3, hull: 3 }`, `AI_FLOAT = { take: 0.5, onward: 0.8, most: 4, reach: 18, fishers: 4, hands: 50, blast: 12, flee: 16, after: 480, stay: 420 }`, the Pontoon, the tow, the three roads.
- **Tests:** `tests/floating.test.ts` (12), `tests/ai-floating.test.ts` (2), `tests/slow/ai-floating-road.test.ts` (1, about 140 s).
