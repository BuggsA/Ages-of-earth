# ai-ab can start both seats in a side age

`scripts/ai-ab.ts` 7th argument now takes `age` (e.g. `{"startEpoch":4,"age":"aeronaut","rich":3000}`), so A/B matches reach the Age of Aeronauts and the forward Airfield's fighter guard (#393, `fieldGuard`) can be judged.

First result: `ai-ab.ts 1 6 15 '{"fieldGuard":false}' '{}' <setup>` → new 6–6 of 12, gathered ×1.002 (se 0.115). No measurable win-rate effect from the guard at this sample; more seeds or longer matches would be needed to say more.

Next: try `--seeds` 20+, or a setup where one side has bombers earlier. No game numbers changed.
