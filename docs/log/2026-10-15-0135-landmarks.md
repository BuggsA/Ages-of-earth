# Contested landmarks (2026-10-15, own pitch #182)

Answering the owner's "mechanical race" note: three kinds of landmark (spring, crag, cairn), one more than the players, on the fair line between the towns. Standing alone on one claims it (20 s for one person, up to 3× faster); rivals pull it down, then take it; contested freezes. A kept one is land (borders), and gives healing, sight or population room. The computer claims with a citizen at 8 citizens and retakes with up to 5 soldiers between waves (Normal/Hard).

Numbers (`src/sim/data.ts`): `LANDMARKS` (max 5, home 22, far 34, fair 4, spacing 16, reach 3.5, claim 20 s, crowd 3, border 5, clear 1, score 100; spring 6 tiles / 3% per 2 s; crag 14; cairn +5) and `AI_MARK` (townAt 8, near 44, rival 0.75, walk 150 s, party 3, guard 5, hold 120 s).
