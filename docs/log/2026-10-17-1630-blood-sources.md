# Beasts, flocks and captives as blood (#355, s1's leftovers)

Chain 1017-1547, session 2. Branch `claude/355-blood-sources`.

- Beasts slain within `BLOOD.reach` of the slayer's rig (or rite altar) give `BLOOD.beast` of their meat as blood (`drawBeast`, called from `hitAnimal`).
- A rig bleeds its people's grown sheep within `BLOOD.flockReach`: up to `flockMilk` milk a second each, `flockYield` blood per milk (`bloodStep`).
- An enemy citizen struck down by a soldier (credit unit of a `GRAFTABLE` type) within reach of a rig or altar with room is taken captive (`Building.captives`), bled `BLOOD.bleed` a second until `BLOOD.captive` are spent, and freed as citizens of its people when the building falls (`freeCaptives` in `removeDead`).
- The computer's machines count a rig or altar holding captives as blood work (`bloodWork` in `ai.ts`).
- HUD: rig/altar panel lines for the bled flock and the captives; toasts; `shackle`/`unshackle` sounds; mist and sparks in the view.
- Numbers added to `BLOOD`: beast 0.2, wolf 8, flockReach 6, flockMilk 2, flockYield 0.6, hold 6, captive 45, bleed 1. No save bump: new fields are optional.
