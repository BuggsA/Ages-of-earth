# The polis (#501, the Greeks)

- Greek citizens are the army: `arm`/`disarm` commands (`src/sim/polis.ts`), H and L buttons, `barred` refuses Greek infantry at the Barracks. Arms cost `POLIS.arm` (20 wood); arms are laid down within `POLIS.home` (14) tiles of a Town Center. No new state (a unit is swapped for another kind, as the call-up does), so no save bump.
- The computer (`polisArms`, ai.ts) arms the citizens nearest a raid, or the ones nearest home when a wave is due and they would fill it; it keeps `POLIS.aiKeep` at work and arms at most `POLIS.aiShare`; answers raids of `POLIS.aiThreat` or more; it disarms only the citizens it armed (`Unit.levy`), idle at home, `POLIS.aiRest` (90) seconds after arming.
- `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` gained lines for the two new commands, as that test asks of every new command.
- The polis may still hire foreign foot at the Market in the Age of Merchants (`barred(p, unit, at)`), as Greek cities hired mercenaries.
- A/B, both computers on Hard, 8 seeds (Romans and Britons, seeds 1–4), at 20 minutes: Greek epochs summed 10 without the polis and 8 with it; peak armies are larger with it (37–40 against 31–48 on the winning seeds, 10–21 against 6–10 on the losing ones). Some seeds still churn (arming 76 times on Romans seed 2).
