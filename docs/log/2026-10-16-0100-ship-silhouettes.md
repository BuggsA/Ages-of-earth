# Ship silhouettes (#267, lane look)

Stone Age war ship is now a war canoe (carved prow, paddlers, team shields, no sail) instead of a copy of the Bronze trireme; the Stone Age transport is a log raft with a hide sail; the Gunpowder transport (and so its merchant ship) is a fluyt with a raised stern, gilded lantern and bowsprit. Aircraft exist only in epoch 4, so there was nothing to vary. Seen in the headless unit gallery (`docs/screenshots/gallery-units-sea.jpg`); in-match look unseen. Render only; no save bump.
