# Thralls: the Age of the Warband takes captives (#502)

**Decision it gives the player:** raid the rival's workers for hands, or leave them be. Taking a citizen swells your labour (up to 8 thralls, none who build) and costs the rival a worker; the rival answers by guarding citizens, walling fields or killing thralls.

**Verbs rubric (`docs/verbs.md`):** (1) verb: a slain rival citizen becomes your own worker, so you hunt workers, not only armies. (2) door: thralls never build or repair, they take pop room, and plunder falls from a quarter to a sixth of a victim's price. (3) two players: raid early for hands, or turtle and keep the Warband for the climb to Iron and Blood. (4) answer: visible dust at the spot; the rival can protect or kill them.

**Changed in `data.ts`:** `AGES.warband.add.plunder` 0.25 → 0.15 (later ages still add 0.25); new `THRALL = { max: 8, hp: 0.6, reach: 14 }`; `AgeDef.thralls`.

**Files:** `src/sim/thralls.ts` (new), `combat.ts` (one call in `plunder`), `units.ts` (`buildStep`), `commands.ts` (`assignBuilders`), `types.ts` (`Unit.thrall`, event `thrall`), `render/view.ts` (dust), `ui/tips.ts`, `README.md`, `tests/thralls.test.ts`. No save change beyond an optional field.
