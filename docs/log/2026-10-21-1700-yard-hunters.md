# The yard hunters: more of them, and they keep clear of soldiers (2026-10-21, Claude Code)

#546: `ai-turning-year` ("in the Deep Wood it hunts the yarded deer") was red on `main` since the Greek harvest levy (#544) moved the Greek wave into winter. Seed 8, Romans: 135 meat that winter, the test wants 140.

**What I saw (seed 8, Romans, Hearth Way).** Five hunters set out once the yard deer were within 16 tiles (about a third into winter) and brought in about 2 meat a second. The Greek wave (4 foot, 5 archers) came in from the side the kills lay on (about 14 tiles out), shot 3 hunters and rang the bell: no meat from 115 s to 140 s. In other runs the bell rang before anyone died and the town stayed inside to the end of winter. The yard here is a single open tile in thick wood, and the Hearth raises no Hunting Camp, so a drop site by the yard was tried and dropped (no room, and barred).

**Changed (`src/sim/ai.ts`, `huntTheYear`):**
- In the yard's winter, with no forager or farmer left to send, a woodcutter (carrying 2 or less) goes to the hunt. A legion town has no fields and its few citizens were on wood.
- A hunter whose deer or carcass has a rival soldier within `AI_WINTER.shy` (8) tiles, carrying 2 or less, goes to wood instead, and no hunter is sent after such deer. This holds on the Herd Plains too (same function).

**Numbers (`src/sim/data.ts`):** `YEAR.yardHunters` 2 → 4 (hunters added in the Deep Wood's winter); new `AI_WINTER.shy` 8.
