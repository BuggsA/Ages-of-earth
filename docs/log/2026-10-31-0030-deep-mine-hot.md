# The deep mine m5: the hot deep, crystal, foul air and the fan (#939, umbrella #924)

- `data.ts`: `STRATA[4]` "The hot deep" (`foul: 45`; every entry gains `foul`), `SHAFT.seams.crystal` (`deep`, `grade: 0.4`), `SHAFT.seams.glowcap.grade` (0.45), `SHAFT` `bears`, `airOpen`, `choke`, `fanAge`, `fan`, `aiFan`; `LODES[].crystal` and `.caps`; `CRYSTAL` (lot 20 buys 100). All new numbers; no old number changed.
- `shafts.ts`: `heldBelow` generalises `boundLayer` to any seam a `STRATA` layer holds a vein of (gems, crystal, glowcaps where no old workings). The air: `hotAt`, `airAt`, `bearsAt`, `atFace`, `breatheStep` (each tick, after `seepStep`: air moves by (at face − bears) / foul seconds), `choke` (dice per miner, `SHAFT.choke`). The fan: `fanProblem`, `raiseFan`, `fanning`; `ventilated` = fan or `forgeDrives` (forge.ts). `placeFor` takes only as many as the air bears unless the miner was sent by hand (`order.hand`, set by `toLayer`); skips a foul layer and anything below a flooded one (`cutOff`). `deepenProblem` refuses below a flooded layer; `floodOut` also drives up those below.
- Crystal rides as stone with `carryRare: 'crystal'`, pays into `Player.crystal`, and never spills as a cache. `trade` with `crystal: true` barters a lot for food, wood or stone at `MARKET.start`. New command `fan` (a line in `tests/command-ownership.test.ts`). New event `choke`.
- State: `Building.air`, `Building.fan`, `Player.crystal`, `Order.hand`, all optional. No `v` bump.
- Computer: raises a fan at a shaft in the hot deep or about to sink into it (unless a forge draws its air), sets one shaft to crystal once it has a Market, and barters each lot for its smallest stock.
- Look: cut-away (ember glow, heat shimmer, sallow haze, crystal shards, glowcaps, the fan house and air pipe, tips), core column (ember ring, shards, caps), pithead model (variant +512: fan house, drum, flared chimney; ember glow down a hot shaft), crystal in the resource bar, Barter buttons at the Market, Raise fan (V), the crystal and glowcap seam buttons, the status line's air, the choke sound, effect and toast, a tip.
- Tests: `tests/deep-mine-hot.test.ts` (14).
