# Ways balance (#486, chain 1017-1808 s2)

Balance pass on the three Ways after they gained verbs (#467). New script: `scripts/ways-sweep.ts` (hard v hard, one line a match, `sum` tots runs up; a treaty option judges economies alone in seconds).

**The measuring trap:** a forced sweep that lets the seed pick the peoples always gives the first-named Way the Chinese, Egyptians or Romans and the second the Britons, Greeks or Mongols (`civ = (seed + i·7919) % 6`, seats swapped by seed parity). Every forced comparison before this measured Way and people together. `ways-sweep.ts` now gives both seats the seed's people in forced mode.

**What was wrong, and the fixes** (each traced on a seed, then swept; small, 25 min, seeds 31–78, homelands cycled, same people both seats):
- *The Hearth's kin* (`think`): from 8 citizens it trained none alone and saved for the call, but out of winter two kin cost 60 food a head against 50. Now out of winter it calls the kin when it holds the food and trains a citizen when it doesn't; only in a Stone Age winter (3 for 120) does it save for the call. (A first try kept 150 food spare before a call: it grew faster but called the kin twice in ten minutes, and `tests/slow/openings-part.test.ts` (#481) rightly wants the verb used.)
- *Winter fields with a full larder* (`planBuildings`, `AI_WINTER.larder` 500): every clever computer raised fields in autumn whatever its store; a Warband with 1,200 food raised 10 farms in a minute with the 200 wood Bronze wanted. Farm-bound Ways (Hearth, Warband) lost most to it; the Hunters, barred from Stone Age farms, escaped it. People still turn to food in autumn (`ai-winter`); only the fields wait.
- *Raid guard* (`rivalCamp`, raid mode): a larder with an armed rival unit within `AI_PROWL.guard` is passed over. Test: `tests/ways-balance.test.ts`.
- *Numbers:* Hearth `wood ×1.1` (new: its farms and houses eat the Stone Age wood that the Hunters' free camps save). `AI_WINTER.larder` 500 (new).

**`main` moved under this work.** On bd6b634 the order was Hunters > Warband > Hearth (Hearth v Hunters 0.30, Hearth v Warband 0.36, Warband v Hunters 0.38), and these fixes plus Hearth wood ×1.1 brought it to 0.48 / 0.61 / 0.39. By cf08735 (snares, breeding herds, the computer sparing wild pairs, thralls) the Hunters had fallen back and the Warband led. The final judgement is against cf08735:

| Pairing (score of first) | `main` cf08735 | AI fixes only | AI fixes + Hearth wood ×1.1 (this PR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hearth v Hunters | 0.52 | 0.56 | 0.59 |
| Hearth v Warband | 0.36 | 0.38 | 0.48 |
| Warband v Hunters | 0.55 | 0.51 | 0.51 |
| each Way's average | 0.44–0.60 | 0.47–0.57 | 0.45–0.54 |

**Held back, and why.** The slow tier pins seeded matches (`lords`, `openings-part`, `landmarks`, `ai-winter`, `ai-web`, `ai-turning-year`, `homelands-ai`), and any Way number moves them. On bd6b634 the Warband was weakest and its best lever, berries and hunting ×0.85 → ×0.95, broke `lords` at every value tried; on cf08735 it no longer needs one. A computer Hunter on the Herd Plains that spares its home herd (#479) may raise no Farm in the Stone Age while it grazes; hunting the herd out when nothing else feeds it fixed a starving town but breaks `ai-turning-year`, which wants the herd kept. That one is a follow-up.

Tried and dropped (no effect beyond noise): Hunters hunt ×1.15 (with berries ×0.85), kin at 100 food, Hearth berries ×1.35, fewer computer hunters and camps, a later Warband band (Barracks at 10, band at 14), Warband +2 melee, raid take 0.5.
