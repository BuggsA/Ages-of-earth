# 2026-10-13: relics (#4)

**Asked:** nothing waiting on the board; the first session of chain 1013-1734 pitched its own work (`AGENTS.md` §2).

**Built:** relics. The map generator places one per town at the same distance (`RELIC.home`, 20 tiles, nearer that town than any other, reachable from it without cutting trees) and `RELIC.extra` more where the land is about as far from its two nearest towns, reachable from every town; they roll their own dice, so the rest of every map is unchanged. Priests fetch them (`relic` command and order), carry them to a Temple (right-click it, or they go to the nearest), and can set them down (`dropRelic`, **D**). A kept relic pays its Temple's owner 1 gold every 2 seconds. A fallen carrier lets go of theirs, a fallen Temple spills its own, and nothing can be built over one. Keeping every relic for `wonderTime` wins (`relicTeam`, `relicTimer`, `how: 'relics'`). The computer builds a Temple early when relics lie loose, trains a priest for them, fetches them, and goes for the temples of a side that keeps them all. View: `src/render/relicsView.ts` (casket on a plinth, carried over the head, circling above the Temple), glitter, picking, effects; sounds `relicTake` and `relicKeep`; HUD relic panel, Temple count, carrying status, the **D** button, the countdown; minimap diamonds; a tip; the end screen's words.
