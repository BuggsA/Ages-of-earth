# Finds in scouting range (#588, #555 s9)

Branch `claude/ecstatic-franklin-c6uf4x`.

- **Sim:** `Find` and `Lead` in `types.ts`; `FINDS`, `AI_FIND` in `data.ts`; `src/sim/finds.ts` (`findStep`, `nearestOf`, `leadOf`); `led` in `ages.ts`, read by `roadKnown` for the survey road only. `placeFinds` in `mapgen.ts` runs last on its own dice and touches no tile; ids from `FINDS.idBase`. Older seeds place everything else as before; saves gain optional `finds` and `leads`; no `v` bump.
- **Hint:** a find names a key the world holds (dealt from a shuffled hand); a world that holds none gets a keyless "hunters' stash". A lead to bog iron shows the iron road without a lodge; other keys inform only (minimap ring, banner).
- **HUD:** minimap circle (amber standing, finder-coloured once claimed) and dashed ring at the nearest key tile; banner and rival toast in `main.ts`, `ruinFind` sound.
- **Computer:** `findWalk` in `ai.ts` sends one citizen once the town has 8 citizens and no danger is near; recalled when the find is taken.
- **Fairness:** over 540 maps (small/medium/large, 2-4 players) about 2% of cramped small 3-4 player maps could not place a find for every town, and spread was wide (up to 5.7 tiles) on a few small lakes/sea maps. Medium and large stayed within 1.8 tiles.
