# Sortie rings (#356, lane look)

Selecting an own aircraft in the Age of Aeronauts draws the fuel bands (`SORTIE.rearm`, `turn`, `reach`) round the nearest three finished own Airfields, through the existing `RangeGuides` mesh (`src/render/rangeGuides.ts`, hook in `view.ts` `drawSelection`). Render only: no rules, numbers, saves or checksum change. `tests/sortie-rings.test.ts` (4 tests) covers the distances, the age/ownership/finished filters, nearest-first with id ties, and the budget of nine guides.

Unseen: the look in a browser (ring colours against terrain, readability at high zoom). Session was started by the owner (chain position 0); it started a look-lane successor per `docs/successors.md`.
