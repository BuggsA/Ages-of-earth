# The computer scouts and keeps watch (#454 s4)

Branch `claude/454-ai-scouts` (on top of #470). A clever computer, after `AI_SCOUT.after` (150 s), sends one idle rider on `explore` (re-sent after `rest` if it stops) and, with 8+ idle soldiers, posts two foot or bowmen on `patrol`: along its trade road to the nearest post (`post`, needs a built Market), else 16 tiles out toward its rival. They rejoin the waves when one sets out. `scouts: false` in `AiLevel` switches it off for `scripts/ai-ab.ts`. Test: `tests/ai-scouts.test.ts`.
