# Every age wears its own interface (rulings 2026-10-25)

- **The owner:** "When I mention a different look each age I mean a real different look. Not a color change, the button icons for upgrades and making people should look different … iconic to the age." #803 had only repainted buildings and hats. Recorded in `docs/rulings.md` and `AGENTS.md` §2 so every agent reads it.
- **Built:** `src/ui/ageIcons.ts`: for all 44 ages a plate shape, a motif and a glyph stamp; the command panel wears them (`plateFor` in `hud.ts`, rules at the end of `style.css`). `unitPicture` and `buildingPicture` take the age, so the buttons, portraits and queue show the age's own models.
- **Test:** `tests/age-icons.test.ts` (all ages dressed, no two share plate and motif, no plate on more than three ages, ink contrasts with the plate).
- **Seen** in Chromium: a contact sheet of all 44 ages, and the live HUD in the Stone Age and the Age of Dykes (plates, motifs, tinted glyphs, the Dykes' Town Center model). Not seen: every age in a live match, touch devices, Low setting frame rate.
- **Not done:** the research queue's glyphs, the age-up preview, the minimap and top bar; per-age hand-drawn glyphs.
- Decision it gives the player: none: look (a rival's road and your own age read at a glance in the button row).
