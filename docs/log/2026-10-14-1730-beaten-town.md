- **Shipped** (`src/sim/ai.ts`; new numbers in `src/sim/data.ts`, new optional `AiMemory.short` in `src/sim/types.ts`, no save bump):
  - **Army-first runs out** (`AiLevel.armyWait`, 90 s at every level). Once its first wave has gone out, an army short of the next wave holds the next age back only `armyWait` seconds in each age (`mem.short`). Before, a town that lost its first battle at 7–9 minutes kept losing every soldier it trained to the rival at its gates and stayed in its age for ten minutes and more, food-starved. The opening is unchanged: before the first wave, army-first holds as it always did.
  - **The bell comes down for stragglers** (`AiLevel.bell`: rings at 5, comes down at 2 or fewer after 20 s). It used to wait for no enemy at all: on seed 21 a beaten Greek town kept 15 of its 35 citizens inside the Town Center from minute 9 to 18 while one to seven of the rival's soldiers came and went.
  - **Patience for a deed nearly done** (`AI_WEB.close`, 0.75): with every deed three-quarters done, the hold lasts twice `patience`. Seed 45: a Greek town gave up its dig at 135 bog iron of 150 with the Iron Age's price banked; now it reaches the Iron Age at 9.5 minutes.
  - **Patience counts only while it holds:** `mem.waiting` is cleared whenever the Town Center isn't ready to climb (army-first, too few citizens), so a hold broken off by army-first doesn't come back already out of patience.
  - `scripts/web-walk.ts duel` now weighs gathering: the walker's over its rival's, and over its trunk twin's (the same people, seat and seed in the seed's other match), overall and by people; a ninth argument sets the first seat's people (`greeks`); a `match {…}` line a match for totting up parallel runs.
  - `scripts/ai-ab.ts` (new): one seat plays Hard as it stands, the other Hard with some `AI_LEVELS` numbers set back, each seed twice with seats swapped. The way to judge a change that affects both sides of a duel (army-first, the bell), which `web-walk duel` can't.
  - `tests/ai-beaten.test.ts`: army-first still holds for the first wave; a beaten town climbs after `armyWait`; the bell comes down with two left; a three-quarters-dug deed outlasts `patience`, and gives up after twice it.
