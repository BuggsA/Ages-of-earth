# The Cockler's Hut, and shore waits that hold their ground (#779, s6e of #701)

Chain 1024-1330, session 5 of 8, lane rules, area:tides (hard). Follows #767 (cockle beds, PR #778).

- **The hut:** `cockler` in `BUILDINGS` (size 2, 350 hp, 60 wood, 15 s, epoch 5, `ages: ['tides']`, eco key **C**). `COCKLER = { reach: 6, wait: 10, regrow: 40 }`. Placement: `cocklerSpot` in `placeProblem` (`'beds'`, refused with "A Cockler's Hut goes within 6 tiles of a cockle bed"). `hutWorks` = built, standing, not asleep.
- **Drop:** `cocklerDrop(s, u, drop)` wraps `nearestDrop` in `dropOff` (`units.ts`): a citizen on a cockle gather order with a food basket goes to the nearest working hut when it is nearer. `drop: []`, so nothing else drops there.
- **Waiting by it:** `hutWait` in `tideWorkHold`: a picker whose bed is under water waits on a dry, walkable tile in the ring round the nearest working hut within `COCKLER.wait` tiles of the bed (one of the three nearest the bed, by `u.id`), else the old shore spot.
- **Tending:** `regrowCockles` gives beds within `COCKLER.reach` of a working hut (`tendedBeds`) `COCKLER.regrow` instead of `COCKLE.regrow`.
- **Homeward:** `homeward(u, o)` replaces the two `o.phase = 'drop'` in `tideWorkHold`: also drops the path, `wantPath` and `tideWait`. Seed 3 showed pickers frozen with baskets for 60 s+ (a shore wait to cross to the bed outlived the turn for home).
- **Shore waits:** `holdShore(s, u)` after `collide` in `game.ts`: a land unit waiting (`tideWait` live with `wantPath`, or `ashore` live), standing still or within 1.5 tiles of the end of its path, that stood on dry ground as the tick began is slid back along the edge of tidal ground (or put back on the tile it stood on). Seed 4's three soldiers had waited on tile (72,42) and been pushed onto (72,43), level 3, swept at high water; in the test map, a soldier still walking to the spot was pushed off it by the crowd until `checkStuck` called it arrived on the flats.
- **Computer:** `cocklerHutWork` in `ai.ts` (from `cockleWork`): busiest bed (most pickers, then lowest tile) more than `AI_COCKLER.far` (12) tiles from its nearest food store and no hut of its within reach: raise one at `cocklerSpotNear`, every `AI_COCKLER.every` (30) s at most.
- **Keys:** hut **C** (free in the Age of Tides: the Caravanserai's C is Silk Road only); Causeway **V → W** (V is the Hive's, W the Windmill's, Age of Dykes only). Tips read the keys from `BUILDINGS`.
- **HUD/look:** `cocklerHut` model (`tideModels.ts`): driftwood planks on posts, thatch, baskets of shells, rake, drying net, lantern, owner's cloth. Panel line "🐚 Tends N beds". Tip `cocklerHut`. Dev hook `ages.cocklerHut(0)`.
- **Measured:** `npx tsx scripts/cockle-probe.ts 6 12` (now also counts huts): swept 4 → 1 over six seeds (the 3 waiting soldiers on seed 4 gone; 1 explorer left, not waiting); huts raised on 4 of 6 seeds; cockles picked 458 → 487 in all.
- **Tests:** `tests/cockler-hut.test.ts` (9). The crowd test fails with `holdShore` taken out; the homeward test fails without `homeward`.
