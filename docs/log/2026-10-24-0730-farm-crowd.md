# One field, one farmer (#344)

**What:** a new audit, `scripts/town-audit.ts`, plays a patient player's whole town for any people (houses, a storehouse by the woods and one by the mines, a barracks, the Workers panel's **+** for a food/wood/stone/gold split) and counts each time a citizen falls idle on its own. On 3 seeds × 15 min × six peoples, 191 of 530 idles came after a field: the Workers panel's **+Food** sent every idle hand to the same half-built field (it skipped fields being farmed, not fields being raised), so three raised it and two stood idle after.

**Fix:** `reassign` passes over a field someone is raising (`labor.ts`); a builder who finishes a field another builder has already taken goes back to the work it was called off (`farmClaimed` in `units.ts`, the player's people only, so `sim.ts 3 medium 30` keeps checksum 1591387014). Idles after a field: 191 → 4.

**What still idles a player's people** (the audit, after): a picker whose bushes run dry with no free field (233, a real choice: lay fields), the five starting citizens (96). And the Workers panel's **+Food** can't find food for the Britons (snares) or the Mongols (flock): "No free food within reach" 730× and 1544× in the audit. That is the next step.
