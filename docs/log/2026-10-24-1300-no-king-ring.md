# Unselected people no longer wear a ring

- Cause: `drawSelection` (`src/render/view.ts`) drew a thin gold "crown" ring under every king, selected or not, in every mode.
- Fix: the crown is drawn only in Regicide, where tracking the king decides the match. Elsewhere a person has no ring until selected.
- New decision for the player: none: look fix.
- Not seen in a browser; typecheck only (`npx tsc --noEmit` clean). Rally, graft and curse rings are status effects and are unchanged.
