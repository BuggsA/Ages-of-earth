# The tower line: looks, and a lighter raw tower

The owner, 2026-10-24: "make the upgraded towers look different also maybe 250 base health instead".

- `BUILDINGS.tower.hp` 600 → 250. To keep raised towers worth their stone, the steps' hit-point multipliers grow: Guard Tower ×1.25 → ×1.5, Keep ×1.25 → ×1.5, Bastion ×1.2 → ×1.4. A tower's hit points by age: Bronze 313 plain / 469 Guard; Middle Ages Keep 844; Gunpowder Bastion 1378 (1750 before the tower line).
- Each step adds to the model of any epoch (`towerLevel` in `buildingModels.ts`, the tower's `variant` is its level): Guard Tower a braced timber gallery; Keep a stone skirt with merlons and corner buttresses; Bastion four angled star-fort points with a cannon in each. A puff of mortar dust and the research chime when a step finishes (`raised` in `view.ts`).
- Seen: headless Chromium (SwiftShader), `scripts/gallery/towers.html`, every epoch × level → `docs/screenshots/tower-line.jpg`. Not seen in a live match.
