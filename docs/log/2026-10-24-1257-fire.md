# Fire: the Wild route's step 3 (#723)

Chain 1024-1233 s1, branch `claude/723-fire`, lane rules (hard).

- **Rules** (`src/sim/fire.ts`, `FIRE` in `data.ts`): torches for citizens and foot soldiers from the Bronze Age (the `torch` order and command); trees, rival Thornwall roots and rival living buildings (`FIRE.burns`) catch; fires spread once a second in lit order on `roll(s)` against `spread[season]`, at most `run[season]` tiles from the torch; trees fall to ash (`Stool.ash`, regrows after `FIRE.ash`); buildings burn `bld` a second until beaten out (`beat` citizen-seconds within `beatReach`) or burnt (`Building.burnt`: no root); scorch for anyone in the flames. Menders of a burning building beat instead of mending (`buildStep`), and a right-click sends them even at full health. `GameState.fires` is in the checksum.
- **Computer:** `torchGrowth` (soldiers out on campaign torch a rival's growth) and `beatFires` (a citizen to each own fire nobody beats).
- **Look and sound:** `src/render/fireView.ts` (flames, a torch in hand, smoke), an `ash` nature model, cues `fireLit`, `fireCatch`, `fireOut`, `fireAsh`.
- **HUD:** Torch (F) on citizens and foot soldiers, a "Burning" line on a building, toasts, two tips.
- **Tests:** `tests/fire.test.ts` (12). `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` gains the `torch` line its exhaustive table asks for.
- Measured: a torch in a big wood burns about 80 trees in summer, 30 in autumn, 8 in spring, 4 in winter (3 seeds each). A 30-minute headless match keeps `main`'s checksum (1591387014): nothing changes where nothing burns.
