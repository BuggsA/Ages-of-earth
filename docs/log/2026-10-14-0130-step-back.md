# Ages as a web, s3: windows and stepping back (#37, #47)

Branch `claude/47-step-back`; chain 1014-0030, session 3 (hard tier), 2026-10-14 UTC.

- `stepBack` command at a Town Center: to `backTo(p)` (`TRUNK[tier - 1]`), cost `stepBackCost(tier)` = 100 food + 50 gold per tier, 25 s (`STEP_BACK.time`), then `STEP_BACK.cooldown` 300 s before the next (`Player.backAt`). Runs on the advance timer with `Building.back`; `journeyOf` gives the road or the step back for the timer, cancel, and HUD.
- `enterAge(…, back)`: on a step back, techs above the tier move to `Player.suspended`; on any age change, suspended techs at or below the tier return to `p.techs`. Queue items the new age can't train or research are refunded. `computeMods` is unchanged (suspended techs aren't in `p.techs`), so `tests/pilgrimage.test.ts`, which pushes a Middle Ages tech onto a Bronze Age player, keeps passing.
- Windows: `shutRoads` (known side roads whose window is another age at or below the tier), shown greyed (🔒) at the Town Center; "Opens only from the …" generated on side-road tooltips (removed from the two blurbs in `ROADS`).
- `epoch` event carries `back`: dusk-blue ring and falling sparks (`view.ts`), `stepBack` sound (`sound.ts`), "Back to the …" banner listing suspended techs and reopened roads (`main.ts`), a tip (`tips.ts`).
- Saves: `Player.backAt`, `Player.suspended`, `Building.back` are optional. No `v` bump.
