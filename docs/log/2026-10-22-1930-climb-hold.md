# Climb hold: stop training hands once the next age is paid for (#555)

**Finding.** Navy seats "stuck in the Stone Age" (route-ab seeds 103, 104; both Greeks) were not a navy bug. With enough citizens and the Bronze price banked, the town kept queuing citizens up to its target (32 on Hard): they ate the savings and kept the Town Center queue non-empty, which `advance` needs empty. An early raid then caught the town still in the Stone Age.

**Change.** `climbReady` in `src/sim/ai.ts`: no more citizens while the main road's price is in hand and the climb's citizen count is met. `AiLevel.climbHold` (default on) lets `scripts/ai-ab.ts` turn it off.
