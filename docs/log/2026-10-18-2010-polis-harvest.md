# The Greek computer brings the harvest in (#523)

**Decision it gives the player:** none: AI fix. (A Greek rival no longer marches in autumn, so autumn is a window to strike its fields, not its hoplites.)

**Built:** `polisHarvest` in `src/sim/ai.ts`: a polis computer neither arms for a wave nor marches one in `POLIS.harvest` season (2, autumn), unless the plan is all-in. New number: `POLIS.harvest` in `data.ts`. Test: `tests/slow/ai-polis-harvest.test.ts` (fails on `main`, passes here).

**A/B** (Greek Hard vs Roman Hard, small, seeds 1-16, 20 min; Greek side):

| | gathered | citizens | epochs | armings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| main | 149,536 | 241 | 12 | 394 |

**Tried and dropped (churn):** arming a raid only as one levy that can meet it cut armings to 254 but halved citizens (131) and epochs (7): the piecemeal arming is what defends the fields. A gap of 8 s between raid armings gave better economy (gathered 171,399) but more armings (424). Neither kept. Armings stay ~375, so the churn ceiling in #523 is **not** met; its economy goal is.
