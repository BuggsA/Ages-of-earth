# Routes and the age panel (#560, #555 s0+s4)

Every age in `AGES` now names its basic routes (`routes`: plain, army, navy, economy, horse, faith; empty for Iron and the Reclaimers, which only a rare find opens) and what it's good at in one line (`good`), with `gateway` ready for s5. `leadsOn`/`leadsTo` (`src/sim/ages.ts`) derive "leads to" from `ROADS`: roads to a later tier, or a turn off the trunk, never a road back; `leadsTo` lists only what the player knows of or has walked. The Town Center's age-up and shut-road buttons show the panel (route, You now, Good at, It shuts, Leads to) and the button title names a side route. `tests/age-panel.test.ts` checks every age has both fields, the trunk is the plain road, each route is reachable from the Stone Age without a ruin or a survey, and rare-only ages sit on no route.

Seen in Chromium (built game, seed 7): the Bronze Age button's tooltip shows the plain road, Good at, and "Leads to: the Middle Ages, and roads you have not found", with no page errors. A side road's button (and its route tag) wasn't seen: it needs a clue building first. No numbers changed.
