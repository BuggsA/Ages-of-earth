# Crows land (look)

**Decision it gives the player:** none: look/sound/fix. Crows dropping to the ground tells you at a glance that a kill is nearly eaten, so butcher it now or let it go.

- `crows.ts`: `spoilingSites` returns `Site`s with `perch` (0 above 40% of the meat left, 1 below 25%, a blend between). The shader drops each crow to the ground round the carcass, wings folded, hopping now and then.
- Test: `tests/crows.test.ts` covers perch at full, nearly gone and in between.
- Not tried: a browser; the hop timing and landing radius are by eye in code only. Built on PR #520 (caw).
