# The Greek computer's levy, smaller and quicker (#523)

**Decision it gives the player:** none: AI fix. (A Greek rival answers a raid of two at once but keeps four in five of its people at the fields.)

**Built:** `POLIS.aiThreat` 3 → 2, `POLIS.aiShare` 0.5 → 0.2 in `data.ts`. Test: `tests/slow/ai-polis-levy.test.ts` (armed ceiling and citizens floor). Scratch harness: `scripts/polis-ab.ts` (`POLIS` keys overridable on the command line).

**A/B** (Greek Hard vs Roman Hard, small, 20 min, Greek side, summed; seeds 1-16 / 17-32):

| | gathered | citizens | epochs | armed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| main | 162,586 / 183,279 | 264 / 342 | 13 / 21 | 374 / 354 |
| threat 2 | 175,986 / 195,410 | 327 / 414 | 16 / 22 | 390 / 335 |
| threat 2, share 0.25 | 179,129 / 213,893 | 353 / 455 | 17 / 23 | 335 / 366 |
| **threat 2, share 0.2 (kept)** | 177,934 / 213,426 | 342 / 449 | 15 / 25 | 323 / 324 |

Each idea alone first: `aiRest` 90 → 180/300/600 changed nothing (armed identical: the churn is raid arming and the deaths after, not disarm cycles); `aiThreat` 5 and `aiKeep` 12 were worse; `aiThreat` 1 over-armed (453). `aiShare` 0.35 alone: gathered 168,723, armed 345.

Armed over 32 seeds: 728 → 647 (−11%); citizens 606 → 791; epochs 34 → 40. Losses fell (1,855 → 1,770). The issue's "near 10" epochs target is met (it was measured at 8; main here gives 13 over 16 seeds, 20 min).

**Unseen:** not tried in a browser (no visible change). Not tried: the issue's "arm only when raiders outnumber trained archers and cavalry" (`have < count + 2` already counts the whole army).
