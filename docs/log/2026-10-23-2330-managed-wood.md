The last of the six peoples' own loops. - food hands sent to the line when berries run out (otherwise they went to wood and food stopped: seed 6, 2,539 food by minute 16, now 5,048);
- a capped Stone Age line and two camps at most (eight snares and six camps cost the climb 400 wood).
