# Ages as a web, s2: bog iron and the Iron Age (#37, #39)

Branch `claude/39-iron-age`; chain 1014-0030, session 2 (hard tier), 2026-10-14 UTC.

- Bog iron (`R_BOG = 7`): `placeBogIron` in `src/sim/mapgen.ts`, its own dice (`seed ^ 0xb0619e`), on `BOG_IRON.chance` (0.5) of seeds; a 5-tile patch per town about 22 tiles out, nearer that town, walkable from it, with room for the whole patch; all towns or none. Only empty ground changes, so other seeds' maps are unchanged. `generateMap(…, deposits = false)` for campaign scenarios.
- Dug like gold (`GATHER.bog` 0.45, `AMOUNT.bog` 300, mining techs apply), paid in as gold; each unit dug adds to `stats.iron`. Diggers move on to the next bog tile (`order.bog`).
- `survey` tech at the Storehouse (50 food, 50 wood, 15 s), epoch 0; on completion a `survey` event with `found`. `surveyFinds` in `ages.ts` reads `s.bogIron`.
- The Iron Age: road `stone → iron` (food 350, wood 150, 35 s, `found: 'survey'`, deed 150 iron); infantry ×0.75 price, +1 attack (new mod `infantryAttack`), farm and wood ×1.1; no cavalry (`Age.no`). Road out: `iron → middle` at `EPOCHS[2]`'s price.
- Saves: `GameState.bogIron`, `PlayerStats.iron`, `Order.gather.bog` are optional; an older save has no bog iron. No `v` bump.
- Checked: `tests/iron-age.test.ts` (10 tests), quick tier, slow campaign proof, a headless match, headless Chromium.
- Not tried by a person: whether 150 dug iron comes at a good moment, and whether a no-horse infantry age is strong or weak against a Bronze rival.
