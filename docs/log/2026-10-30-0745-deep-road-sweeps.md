# The Deep Road s7: sweeps, the `deep` leaning, the computer walking it to the end (#876, #906)

- Leaning: `settleLean` (`ai.ts`) leans `deep` on its own coin (`0xde17`), one share among the open routes, before the other routes are drawn, so a seat that doesn't lean deep keeps its old dice. Over 240 Hard seats (60 seeds, continental and lakes, 2 seats) 16 leaned deep; never the Mongols. A deep seat sets every Deep Road age `true` and the Stone Age's other side turns `false`.
