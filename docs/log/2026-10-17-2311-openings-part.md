# Two openings part ways: the proof (#481, Goal 1 s4)

`scripts/opening.ts` now exports its runner (`playOpening`, `parting`) and reads off each town's food by source (berries, meat, fish, fields, snares, milk, raids), its work mix (citizen-seconds by job, fishing boats as fish), how far out its food workers go, kin called, and its own home-herd deer alive minute by minute. The CLI prints all of it, the mixes in five-minute windows, and takes `hunt:feast` (that town hunts its own home herds out, two citizens at a time, as a player who feasts would). No rule or AI change; no numbers changed.

`tests/slow/openings-part.test.ts` (about 2 minutes) plays the same seat under two Ways on the same seed (the rival swaps with it, so map, start and neighbour are held), seeds 3 and 8, small maps, four comparisons a homeland:

| Homeland | Pair | Every comparison | At least 3 of 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Glade, Shore, Herd Plains | Hunters vs Hearth | first four buildings differ; the Hearth raises no Hunting Camp and calls 4+ kin, the Hunters none; more of the Hunters' food in minutes 0–10 is meat | Hunters' camp among their first four by minute 5; more of the Hearth's food is fields; the Hunters' food workers range farther |
| Deep Wood | Warband vs Hunters | the Warband's first building is a Barracks, before the Hunters' one; the Hunters camp by minute 2.5 and have more meat by minute 5 and a bigger meat share | the Warband raids food; its food workers stay nearer home |
| Herd Plains | Hunters sparing vs feasting (13 min) | the feaster's home herd is gone by minute 5 and its first Farm stands before the sparer's; the sparer still has deer at minute 10 and butchers more meat in the second spring (minutes 10–13) | |

Thresholds come from a matrix of 108 small matches (3 Ways × 3 Ways × 4 homelands × seeds 3, 8, 11) and 12 feast matches (seeds 3, 5, 8, 11): an "every" claim held in all six same-seat comparisons per homeland, a "3 of 4" claim slipped once. Second spring, Hunters: sparer 269–1145 meat, feaster 0–10, on all 8 runs.

Findings for the owner and the ai lane (#486):
- **Mix distances don't prove much on their own.** Two towns on the same Way, at different spots on the map, differ about as much as two Ways do, so the test compares seats, not towns.
- **The Hunters part clearly from both other Ways** (same-seat food-mix gap over minutes 0–10: 15–42% on average, Shore lowest, against a 6–14% noise floor, where only the rival's Way changed).
- **The Hearth and the Warband barely part on the land.** Their food and work mixes differ no more than the noise (Shore 7% vs 7%, Deep Wood 12% vs 11%, Herd Plains 18% vs 14%); only the verbs set them apart (kin, a Barracks at 0.3 min, raids).
- **The Warband on the Shore** often raises no Barracks in 15 minutes (barracks 4.6 min to never across 18 runs), and its raids miss on 8 of 18. So the Deep Wood carries the Warband case in the test, not the Shore.
- **A Hearth town on one Herd Plains seed (11)** fed more in the second spring when it feasted, since its spared herd (2 deer) didn't calve. The feast case uses the Hunters.

Not tried: medium or large maps in the test (the CLI defaults to medium; the matrix was small), human play. The owner is asked to play two openings on #463.
