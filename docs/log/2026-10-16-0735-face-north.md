# A match starts facing north (#345)

The owner: the minimap "feels like the opposite of the world: the top-right corner of the minimap reads as the bottom-left corner of the view".

Cause: the title screen's attract camera turns 0.05 rad a second (`src/main.ts`, the title loop) and `startMatch` never reset the turn, so a match opened at whatever way the title had drifted: about 170° after a minute. The minimap is always drawn north-up, so the two disagreed, top-right ↔ bottom-left. Fix: `CameraRig.faceNorth()` (`src/render/camera.ts`) sets the yaw and its goal to 0, called in `startMatch`. `[` and `]` still turn the camera and Backspace turns it back.

Tried in headless Chromium: waited 60 s on the title then started a match, before and after the change. Before, the Town Center and the minimap's view box sat skewed; after, square on. No console errors.

Not done: the minimap could turn with the camera (or draw a north arrow) for players who turn with `[`/`]`. If the owner still finds it inverted after this, that's the next step.
