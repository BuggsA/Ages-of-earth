# Routes that read the tide (#755, s6c of #701)

Chain 1024-1330, session 3 of 8, lane rules, area:tides (hard). Follows #748 (barrages, PR #754).

- **The check:** `tideRoute(s, u, team, budget)` in `src/sim/tides.ts`, called by `routeUnits` (`units.ts`) after each land route is laid (the straight-line shortcut and `findRoute` both). Only while the tide is awake; otherwise it returns at once, so nothing changes off the Age of Tides. `passage` walks the route's waypoints in quarter-tile samples: each tidal tile stepped on (bared, no building), the first one `safeOver` fails (pond, or not `bareThrough` its tidal level from arrival to `1.5 / pace + TIDE_ROUTE.margin` s after; pace = speed / `TIDE_ROUTE.slack`), and the last dry tile before it (`stop`; the unit's own spot if it stands on dry ground). The tile under the unit is never doomed.
- **Round:** `findPath` gains an optional `veto(tile, g)` (`path.ts`): asked on each step, and on a diagonal step's two corner tiles, with `g` the grid distance walked; `smooth` pulls the route taut only where `lineSafe` (the same veto along the straight line) allows. The round route is checked again with `passage`.
- **Wait:** `ebbFrom`: the first tick from reaching `stop`, within `TIDE_ROUTE.hold` s, at which every tidal tile beyond `stop` stays bare from setting out until past it. The route is cut at `stop`, `wantPath` stays true and `Unit.tideWait` holds the tick; `routeUnits` skips the unit until then. `goTo` clears `tideWait`. Event `tideHold`.
- **Choice:** round if it arrives no later than waiting would; else wait; else the route as laid.
- **On the way:** `rereadRoutes` at each turn of the tide sets `wantPath` for a walking land unit whose route ahead now fails.
- **HUD and look:** unit panel chip 🌊 Tide Ns (`tideWaitLeft`); toast (at most every 30 s); tip `tideRoute`; a lap of foam at the stop for the viewer's units (`view.ts`).
- **Save:** no `v` bump: `tideWait` is optional, absent in older saves.
- **Numbers new in data.ts:** `TIDE_ROUTE = { slack: 1.25, margin: 4, hold: 180 }`.
- **Measured:** `npx tsx scripts/tide-probe.ts 6 12` (three hard computers in the Age of Tides, lakes, medium, 12 min): sweeps by seed 0/4/2/4/9/10 on main, 3/0/1/0/0/0 now (the 4 left: builders on causeway sites out on the flats, a gatherer, a cavalryman stuck in a crowd on the flats for a minute). Matches take the same time (9–13 s each).
- **Tests:** `tests/tide-routes.test.ts` (5), `tests/slow/ai-tide-routes.test.ts` (1, ~28 s).
- **Not done:** cockle beds (new issue); builders and gatherers working out on the flats don't read the tide while they work.
