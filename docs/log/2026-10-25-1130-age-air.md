# The air of every age (#803 s3)

The owner, 2026-10-25: "each age should have a distinct or unique look." Buildings and people were dressed (s1, s2); the light was not.

- **Built:** `src/render/ageAir.ts`, a tint and strength (0.1 to 0.4) for all 43 ages. `view.ts` eases the viewer's age's air in (`easeAir`) and lays it over the sky top, the horizon and fog (1.3 times as far), the sun's colour and the hemisphere light, before weather greys it. Drawn only; no rule changes.
- **Test:** `tests/age-air.test.ts`: every age has one, strength within bounds, no two noon horizons closer than 8 in RGB (four tints retuned to pass: merchant, floating, tides, and a second tides/searaider pair).
- **Seen:** `scripts/look/airs.mjs` in Chromium, ages stone, industrial, mycelium, hive, in rain. The first pass (sky and sun only) was nearly invisible from the default camera, so the hemisphere light leans too; now the mycelium town reads cooler and violet, the hive warmer. Still subtle in rain; not seen in clear weather, at night, or for the other 39 ages.
- **Decision it gives the player:** none: look.
- **Next:** the ground round a town; riders and ships that wear their age; stronger tints if the owner wants them (they are one number each).
