# The Wild route, step 1: the Age of the Wild

The owner, 2026-10-24: "start a new main branch of ages. Completely new … nature/plant tech. Where you use nature and animals to your advantage." Then: produce the beasts too, tree walls, ents later; denser food; "very uniquely creative" future ages; "this goes to 7".

- **Design:** `docs/wild-road.md`. Seven ages of its own: Wild, Grove, Hive, Wildwood, then a fork at the top (Leviathans or the Bloom), and the rare Mycelium gateway; plus the Wild Hunt crossing with the Steppe. Tree walls (Thornwall), dense food (Kitchen Gardens fed by Den dung or water), Ents, Skywhales, a Kraken, the Behemoth, the Bloom Seed and the Greening victory are all specified there, with steps.
- **Built:** the Age of the Wild (tier 1, from the Stone Age; clue: a Hunting Camp; deed: 250 food foraged from wild bushes, a new `foraged` stat). Route `wild`.
- **Taming** (`src/sim/tame.ts`, `TAME`): a citizen right-clicked on a wild wolf, boar or mammoth sings it calm (6 / 10 / 24 s) and it becomes a War Wolf (cavalry class), Tusker (infantry class) or War Mammoth (siege class, hits units too). New `tame` order and command. The beast stands calm for its tamer (`Animal.tamer`, `calmFor`), its pack doesn't; a wound breaks the song, and a beast hurt within 8 s won't take the hand.
- **Beast Den** (`BUILDINGS.den`, 50 food + 75 wood): raised only once a beast is tamed; breeds only the tamed kinds (`Player.tamed`, checked in `barred`); keeps breeding in any later age.
- **The call of the wild** (`callWild`): 20 s into each season, a Wild people's finished Hunting Camp draws a boar (spring, summer) or a lone wolf (autumn, winter) from 7 tiles out, up to 2 wild beasts near it. Added after a headless match showed the computer had hunted every beast near home by the time it reached the Wild.
- **Door:** no Stables, no Siege Workshop while in the Wild (`noBuild`).
- **Look and sound:** three beast models (the wild model in its people's collar, saddle-cloth or howdah), the Den model, a hummed lullaby (`tame`) and a green ring when a beast is tamed; web-map lane 4.
- **The computer** (`wildRoad` in `ai.ts`): takes the road on its own coin (`TAME.aiTake` 0.3; it shifts no other dice), raises the Hunting Camp once 80% of the deed is in, spares mammoths, boars and wolves while it tames, sends one tamer at a time (a wolf only with 4 soldiers sent ahead at its pack), raises a Den (it may spend what it holds for the climb), stays 150 s. Peoples whose signature is riders or siege keep away.
- `tests/wild.test.ts` (11). `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` gains the `tame` row its type demands of every new command.
- **Headless scan** (`npx tsx scripts/wild-scan.ts 16 20`): 4 of 32 computers took the Wild; seed 14's Hard tamed 2 wolves, raised a Den, bred 12 beasts and won at 13.2 min; seed 8's Normal tamed 8 and raised a Den but stayed poor in the Wild for 14 minutes (its economy is thin there; step 2's Gardens should help).
