# Areas: several chains on the art at once (#617)

The owner asked that many agents and chains can work on the art together. `docs/team.md` and `docs/successors.md` now let an umbrella split its lane into *areas* with their own hot files: one claim and one chain per area. `docs/art.md` sets the art's bar, five areas (light, ground, units, buildings, overlays) with their files, a working palette that `area:light` owns, and how each step shows before and after. `scripts/look/shots.mjs` takes the same three scenes (`stone-start`, `battle`, `town`) for every area; run against `vite preview` it wrote all three with no page errors. Decision added for the player: none (process).
