# The Water Road, s6: the Age of Tides (#739, #701)

Chain 1024-1330, session 1 of 8, lane rules, area:tides (hard). Built beside chain 1024-2000 (s4, s5) under #739's area split.

- **The tide:** `src/sim/tides.ts`. Asleep (high water, `tideLevel` = `TIDE_TOP`) until a people stands in the Age of Tides; `tideStep` then wakes it (`wakeTide`: `GameState.tidal`, `tideBed`, `tideFrom`) and runs a clock from `s.tideFrom` in integer ticks: steps of `TIDE.period / 6` s, levels 3 → 2 → 1 → 0 → 1 → 2. `tideClock` is the public reading (HUD, computer).
- **Tidal ground:** its own layer, not the polder/flood layer. Laid once, in tile order: open sea (`seaAt`) with no building, shoal, polder or channel, level k = count of `TIDE.flats` at or below the mean corner depth. On each turn `settle` bares (T_SAND, no `B_TERRAIN`, corners raised to `TIDE.dry`) or covers (T_WATER, `B_TERRAIN`, corners back to `tideBed` where the tide raised them and nothing moved them since); tiles under a building, polder, channel or shoal are left alone. `blockVersion` and `tideTurns` bump.
- **Sweep:** land units on a tile the water covered are moved to dry ground above the tide within `TIDE.sweep` (else any bared ground), hurt `TIDE.hurt`, route cleared and asked again; drowned with none. Checked on each turn and every 5 ticks after (for routes laid while the flats were bare).
- **Causeways:** building `causeway` (1 tile, drag as a wall, `wall` command `causeway: true`), on tidal ground or open sea down to `TIDE.causeway`; finished, it becomes `tidal = TIDE_CAUSEWAY` (level 3) and the building is gone, as a ditch is.
- **Tide Mills:** building `tidemill` (2×2, shore, the keepsake). `millShares`: each working mill, in id order, claims up to `mill.most` unclaimed tidal tiles within `mill.reach`; pays `mill.gold × n / most` gold on each turn.
- **Aground:** a Dock with no open water within `TIDE.harbour` tiles finishes no ship (`aground`, checked in `game.ts`'s training).
- **Placement:** `placeProblem` gives `'tidal'` on bared ground (dykes and causeways excepted), and `causewaySpot` for causeways.
- **The age and roads:** `AGES.tides` (tier 5, `routes: ['water']`, verb/door/good, keepsake tidemill, `noBuild: silo`); `atomic → tides` (Dock clue, coast door), `tides → atomic`. Web map lane 5, row 6.
- **The computer:** `wants('tides')` on coin salt 0x71de (`AI_TIDES.take`, `.onward` for a Water Road people); `tideWork` saves the road's price and turns once the Town Center is free (at the last epoch the TC trains hands for ever, so `turnRoad` alone never fired); raises up to `AI_TIDES.mills` mills at `tideMillSpot`; any people steps its units off flats the next step covers, `AI_TIDES.wary` s ahead. `siloRoad` skips a barred silo. Headless (lakes, medium, from the Atomic, 12 min, seeds 1–6): 4 of 18 seats took it; mills ground 300–520 gold.
- **Look and sound:** wet ribbed sand and pale causeway paving in `TerrainView.paint` (repaint on `tideTurns`); `TidesView` draws causeways under the water; Tide Mill and causeway-site models in `src/render/tideModels.ts`, its wheel turning in the windmill's `sails` slot; minimap paints bared flats; sounds `tideWash`, `tideMill`, `swept`; effects for sweeps, milling, causeways.
- **HUD:** the tide clock beside the game clock (arrow, three bars, seconds to the turn; tooltip with low and high water); Tide Mill and Dock status lines; toasts for the waking, low water, the flood with your people on the flats, and sweeps; tips `tides` and `tideAwake`; dev hook `ages.tides(0)`.
- **Save:** no `v` bump. The new state fields are optional and absent in older saves (the tide asleep).
- **Numbers new in data.ts:** `TIDE = { period: 180, flats: [-1.0, -0.75, -0.5], harbour: 2, dry: 0.08, sweep: 6, hurt: 15, causeway: -1.6, mill: { reach: 5, most: 24, gold: 60 } }`, `AI_TIDES = { take: 0.35, onward: 0.7, mills: 3, least: 8, reach: 20, wary: 12 }`; Causeway 4 wood 8 stone, 400 hp, 6 s; Tide Mill 200 wood 150 stone, 900 hp, 35 s; road 800 food 800 gold 450 stone, 60 s; back 300 food 300 gold, 30 s.
