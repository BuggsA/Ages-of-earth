# Verbs in the data (#506)

- Each side age, Way and homeland in `src/sim/data.ts` now has `verb` (what the player now does, in words) and `door` (what it shuts). `Civ` has the same optional fields.
- `tests/verbs.test.ts` fails one that lacks them, or whose verb holds a digit or `%`. Known gaps live in `VERB_GAPS` with their issue: the six peoples (#501), the Age of the Warband (#502), Reclaimers (#503). The test also fails if a gap gains a verb but stays listed.
- The Way buttons and the age-road buttons end their tooltip with "You now: … It shuts: …", so the player reads the choice as a verb too.
- Seen in Chromium: the Hunters' Way tooltip on seed 7 shows the new lines, with no page errors. Not seen: an age-road tooltip (needs a match played to the Bronze Age).
