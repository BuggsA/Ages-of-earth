# Ore at the Market (#356 s5, chain 1016-2345 s4)

**Built.** The `trade` command now takes a rare ore as `res` (sell only): a lot of `ORE_TRADE.lot` (20) pays `ORE_TRADE.gold` (salt 40, saltpetre 55, blood-iron 90) at a flat price, no drift. Buying is refused. Ore sold leaves `p.rare` but not `stats.mined`, so ore doors stay open. HUD: Market panel shows a sell button per ore in hand. AI: `trade()` sells any ore lot first (clever computers with a built Market). I reused `trade` rather than a new command because `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` (read-only) lists every command type.
