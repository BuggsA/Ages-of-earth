# Take it back: key ore knocked loose and carried home (#570, #555 s3)

Chain 1022-1508, session 3. Branch `claude/570-take-back`.

- Rules (`src/sim/caches.ts`): ore stores are land buildings that take stone in (`storesOre`). One that falls spills `CACHE.share` of each rare ore, divided by the stores standing, as an `OreCache` (`GameState.caches`, optional: older saves load with none, so no save `v` bump). Fallen diggers drop their basket, fallen carriers their load; at sea with no shore within 12 tiles it sinks. Citizens lift `CACHE.load` (`Unit.ore`) and bank at their own store, by order (`cache`) or by walking within `CACHE.bank` of one. Spills subtract from `stats.mined`; banking adds to it and to `stats.seized` when it was a rival's.
- Command `cache` (citizens only, `myUnits`), a line in `tests/command-ownership.test.ts`'s every-command table (the table is built to fail to compile without it).
- Computer: `carryCaches` (all levels) and `oreTarget` for the clever wave (`AI_CACHE`).
- Look and sound: `src/render/cacheView.ts`, minimap squares (in sight; all of them once assayed), toasts, unit status, tip, sounds `oreSpill` and `oreHome`.
- Seen in headless Chromium: caches and a spill drawn, rare stock in the top bar, no page errors.
