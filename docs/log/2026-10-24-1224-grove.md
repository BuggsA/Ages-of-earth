# The Wild route, step 2: the Age of the Grove (#721, umbrella #706)

The owner asked this session to continue the nature/plant trunk and make it a chain.

- **Age** `grove`, tier 2, route `wild`, from the Wild (clue: a Beast Den; deed: 5 beasts reared at Dens, a new `bred` stat counted in `bringOut`). Roads on to the Gunpowder Age (trunk price) and back to the Middle Ages (200 food, 200 wood). Door: no stone walls, gates or Watch Towers.
- **Thornwall** (`thornwall`, 5 wood, drag a line like a wall: the `wall` command takes `thorn`). Grows a stage each 40 s, twice as fast in spring (`Building.grown`, `level` 0–3); max hp is `GROVE.hp[stage]` of a wall's (`buildingMaxHp`); living oak mends 3 hp/s once unhurt 5 s. Parts for its own team like a gate (`stampBuilding`). Cut down, it leaves a root (`GameState.roots`) that sprouts a seedling 45 s later unless built over; one its people deletes is grubbed out (`Building.grub`).
- **Kitchen Garden** (`garden`, 1 tile, 35 wood): a field (`isField`) worked at a farmer's pace × 2.2 with Den dung, × 1.6 with water within 3 tiles, × 2.8 with both (`Building.fed`, refreshed each second; a Den feeds its 6 oldest gardens within 6 tiles).
- **Orchard** (`orchard`, 2×2, 100 wood): bears after 120 s, then 3 food per 10 s in summer and autumn while a food store stands within 8 tiles.
- All three raised by a people that walked the Grove, in any age after; hidden from the build menu otherwise.
- **Look and sound:** Thornwall model per stage, joined like a wall; garden shows its dung heap or water jar; orchard young, grown, in fruit. A sprout burst and a green-wood creak when a root regrows. Tip `grove`.
- **Computer** (`groveWork`, `plantGarden`): goes on from the Wild, stays 180 s, plants gardens round its Den before farms, up to 2 Orchards, one Thornwall line of 9 toward the nearest rival.
- `tests/grove-age.test.ts` (13; `tests/grove.test.ts` is the Sacred Grove landmark's). Headless: `npx tsx scripts/wild-scan.ts 16 25` → seed 14's Hard took the Grove, 3 fed gardens, 12 beasts bred, won at 13.1 min; other seeds unchanged in kind (4 took the Wild).
- Browser: dev server, a Grove town built through `window.ages` (a new `thornwall` option on `ages.wall`), screenshots of the stages, gardens and orchard; no console errors. Plinths removed under gardens and hedges.
