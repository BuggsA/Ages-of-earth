# The tower line: weaker raw towers, upgraded at the tower

The owner, 2026-10-24: "The watch towers are too strong. Is there any way to make them a lot weaker and then be able to upgrade them over time. I have a tower build and it is hard to stop them."

- Watch Tower base: hp 1000 → 600, arrow 8 → 5, range 8 → 7, reload 1.8 → 2.0 s. Its arrows gain 10% an epoch by themselves (`GUNS.towerEpoch`), not 20% (Town Centers and Blood Towers keep 20%, `GUNS.epoch`).
- The owner, after the first version: "the towers should be per tower upgrade". So each tower has its own `level` (`Building.level`), raised one step at a time at that tower with the `raiseTower` command (`TOWER_LEVELS`): Guard Tower (Bronze; 100 wood, 50 stone; +2 arrow, ×1.25 hp), Keep (Middle Ages; 100 wood, 125 stone; +2 arrow, +1 range, ×1.25 hp), Bastion (Gunpowder; 100 wood, 75 gold, 175 stone; +2 arrow, ×1.2 hp). The step works in the tower's queue (`TrainItem.rise`) and the tower keeps shooting. The panel names the tower by its level. A first version used three global techs; they are gone.
- One arrow, Bronze Age, no forge techs: 9.6 → 6.0 raw, 8.4 with the Guard Tower. Medieval Keep: 12.6 vs 11.2 before. Gunpowder Bastion: 14.3 vs 12.8 before. Hit points at Bronze: 1250 → 750 raw.
- Coil Towers: the bolt's first strike is `LIGHTNING.bolt` (2) times the arrow, so a raw Industrial coil hits about as before (14 vs 14.4).
- `gunPower` / `gunRange` / `buildingMaxHp` in `stats.ts`, all taking the tower's level, are now the one place guns are computed (combat, battle tide, explorers' wariness, range guides, the building panel).
- Clever computers raise their towers, lowest level first, with `AI_TOWER_KEEP` of each resource to spare (`raiseTowers` in `ai.ts`). New tip `towerLine`. `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` gains the `raiseTower` row its type demands of every new command.
- Not seen in a browser; no new tower models for the upgrades yet (a next step: a taller storey, a stone keep, a low bastion).
