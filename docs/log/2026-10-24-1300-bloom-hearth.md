# The Iron Age gets a building of its own: the Bloom Hearth (#727, first step)

Chain 1024-1237, session 1, area:town of #724. The Iron Age had no keepsake and no building. It now has the **Bloom Hearth** (`bloomery`, key I, Iron Age only, `BUILDINGS` in `data.ts`): a gold drop-site that **smelts** bog iron (each bar hauled to it counts `BLOOMERY.smelt` times toward the forge steps; `deposit` in `units.ts`), trains foot soldiers beside the marsh, and is the age's keepsake (`AGES.iron.keepsake`): kept, it keeps training spearmen at the Iron Age's price (`trainCostFor`, same pattern as the Sea-Raiders' Dock). The computer raises one beside the nearest bog (`raiseBloomery`, `ai.ts`). Model `bloomery` in `buildingModels.ts`; tip `bloomery`; README line. Test `tests/bloomery.test.ts`.

**Decision it gives the player:** haul the iron to a hearth on the marsh (a building to defend, a sharper army) or to the safe Storehouse; and whether to keep the hearth when leaving.

**Why only the Iron Age this time.** Tests pin the shared keepsakes of the ages the brief named first (`tests/keepsakes.test.ts` expects Alchemy's workshop, the Sea-Raiders' and Sail's dock; other files pin the Pilgrims' temple, the Fairs' market, Lightning's tower, the Blood-rite's temple). Tests are read-only, so moving those keepsakes needs a ruling: raised on #727. Ages with no keepsake today (Iron, Merchants, Warband, Steppe, Horde, Chariots, Sappers, Wild, Grove, Aeronauts, Reclaimers, Fission, Fusion, Golden) have no such pin, so they go first.
