# Enough wild to tame (2026-10-30)

The owner asked that the Wild branch be easy to take animals in: enough of them, easy to click, easy to tame.

- **Measured** (`npx tsx scripts/tame-probe.ts 8 35`): at the start, within 35 tiles, a town has ~1 boar, ~1 mammoth and 0.0–0.9 wolves; 750 of 768 town-starts (2 sizes × 6 map types × 4 homelands × 8 seeds × 2 towns) lack at least one kind. The old call brought one boar or wolf a season, never a mammoth.
- **Changed** (`src/sim/tame.ts` `callWild`, `TAME` in `data.ts`): `callEvery` 50 (new; was once a season), `callCap` 2 → 3, `callMammoths` 1 (new). The untamed kind comes first; a mammoth comes while none is tamed, then each winter. Call directions are now unit length, so diagonal calls land inside `callNear`.
- **The computer** (`wildRoad` in `ai.ts`, `TAME.aiHerd` 3, new): with the wild now plentiful it kept a citizen always taming and stalled its Den, so `tests/slow/ai-grove-road.test.ts` (seed 3) failed. Once it holds 3 beasts it tames only a kind it lacks; the slow test passes again, and on seed 3 the Normal computer reaches the Grove at 17 min (19 before).
- **Click:** animals' pick radius follows their size (`src/render/unitsView.ts`), never smaller than before.
- **Tip** for the Age of the Wild mentions the call.
- **Seen:** `tests/tame-supply.test.ts` (new, 6 tests, ~7 s); `tests/wild.test.ts` unchanged and green; `npx tsx scripts/wild-scan.ts 10 20` before/after: every computer that took the Wild now tames a mammoth (before: 1 of 6); seed 7's Normal still reaches the Hive. `npx vitest run --mode slow tests/slow/wild.test.ts tests/slow/ai-grove-road.test.ts` → 14 passed. `npm run check` → 1634 passed (about 320 s in this container; the new file takes about 7 s). Not tried in a browser.

## The teeming wild (same day, the owner: animals more plentiful in general)

- **Built:** `TEEM` (data.ts), `placeTeeming` (mapgen.ts), spawn in `createGame` with ids from `TEEM.idBase`, and the new beasts idle on hashed dice (`beastStep` in animals.ts), as raider camps already do. Each town gets a mammoth at 28 tiles and its own wolf den at 35–41 tiles (only if every town gets one). The open ground gets small/medium/large 2/3/4 more mammoths, 2/3/4 boars (by berries or trees) and 1/2/3 dens.
- **Why this shape:** raising `MAMMOTH`/`BOAR`/`WOLF` counts in `placeWild` shifted every later dice roll of the map and the match's shared dice, and 26 seed-bound tests failed. Laying the new beasts last on their own dice left 3; tuning distances fixed those. The own den sits past `AI_WILD.clear` (32), where the computer no longer sends soldiers by reflex, so a landmark test's escort stays clear of it. The per-town boar is 0, because one 12–22 tiles out charged a test's woodcutter.
- **Computer:** a Grove people skips the far mammoth hunt (`ai.ts`), or its hunting party left it wanting no gardens (`tests/grove-age.test.ts`).
- **Also:** `raiders` placement unchanged. `scripts/wild-watch.ts` no longer crashes when a boar dies.
- **Numbers:** per town within 40 tiles, wolves 0.5–3.4 → 3.1–5.5, mammoths 1.0–1.7 → 2.0–3.5. Deaths to beasts in `wild-watch 6 12 normal` went from about 6.8 to about 11.2 a match.
- **Checks:** `npm run check` → 329 files, 1636 tests passed. The slow tier result is in the PR.
