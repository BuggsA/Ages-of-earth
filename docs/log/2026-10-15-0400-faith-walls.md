# Faith takes the walls (2026-10-15, #209)

The Age of Pilgrims gave up siege and offered nothing in its place. Now a pilgrim people's priests chant a rival's building over: one shared tally per building (`faith`, `faithBy`, `chant` on `Building`), filled by every priest in range at `CONSECRATE.rate` of the building's full health a second (times priest speed), slowed by those sheltering inside, fading after a pause. When it turns, `turnBuilding` refunds the queue to the loser, drops the farmer, rally and beacon, and restamps the footprint so a gate opens for its new side. Town Centers and Wonders never turn.

- Rules: `src/sim/consecrate.ts`; `convertStep` in `units.ts` hands building targets to `consecrateStep`; `commands.ts` lets `convert` and `attack` send priests at buildings; `faithStep` in `game.ts`. A farm's old farmer now stops when the farm isn't theirs.
- Computer: `chantBand` in `ai.ts`, `AI_FAITH` in `data.ts`; a pilgrim computer keeps six priests once it has an army of six.
- Look and sound: motes in `View.ambient`, a ring and gold fountain on `consecrated`, the owner added to the building view's key, the `consecrate` sound.
- HUD: a faith line on the building panel, the convert cursor, toasts, the `consecrate` tip.
- Tests: `tests/consecrate.test.ts` (10).
