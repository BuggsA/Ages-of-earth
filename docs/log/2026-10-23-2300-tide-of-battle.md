# The tide of battle (#606)

Own pitch, lane look. A bar over each fight the player's side is in: their strength against the foe's they can see (Lanchester's square law through `damageOf`), a word (Crushing / Winning / Even / Losing / Routed) and a swing arrow. Click picks up your fighters there. A fight that falls from even under 0.38 out of sight warns: toast, minimap ping, a new `tideTurns` horn, Space looks (at most every 20 s).

- `src/sim/battles.ts`: `battlesFor(s, viewer)`, a pure read (deterministic, changes nothing). Citizens count only while striking or struck; towers and Town Centers count with their garrison's arrows.
- `src/ui/battleTide.ts`: the bars, smoothing and the turn warning; wired in `main.ts`, a tip in `tips.ts`.
- Cost: on a 40-minute hard-vs-hard match (seed 3, medium), 2,695 reads, worst 1.8 ms, typically 0.1 ms.
- Browser (headless Chromium, swiftshader): a 6v3 fight by the Town Center showed "Crushing" over the clash; clicking it selected the 6 soldiers; a 5v5 fight 16 tiles off went Winning → Even ▼ → Losing ▼ as 14 riders arrived with the camera elsewhere, and the toast fired. No page errors. Not heard: the horn's loudness.

Numbers added in `data.ts`: `BATTLE` (new). Decision added for the player: when to pull a fight back or pour into it, read from the bar.
