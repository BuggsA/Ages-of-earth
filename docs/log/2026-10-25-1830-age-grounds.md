# The ground wears your age (#815)

Follows #803 (the owner, 2026-10-25: "each age should have a distinct or unique look"; asked again in the owner's chat with Claude Code the same day).

- **Built:** `src/render/ageGrounds.ts`: a ground for each of the 45 ages (colour, accent, pattern) and `groundColor`. `TerrainView.wearTowns` lays it round each known building (full on the footprint and the ring round it, fading over 3 tiles; lines, fields and saplings left alone), checked every 0.4 s from `view.ts`. Only changed tiles are repainted.
- **Age-up:** `TerrainView.sweepFrom`: tiles changed by an age-up wait until a wave from the Town Center reaches them (6 tiles a second). The sparks of a side age's age-up take its palette (`ageSparks`); the trunk keeps gold, a step back dusk blue.
- **Test:** `tests/age-grounds.test.ts`: every age has a ground; L and S bands; no two alike over an 8×8 patch; sparks.
- **Seen** in Chromium (SwiftShader): all 45 ages, `docs/screenshots/age-grounds-1..4.jpg` (`scripts/gallery/shoot-grounds.mjs`); an age-up to the Grove mid-wave and after, `age-grounds-sweep-a/b.jpg`. Toned down after looking: Fusion, Atomic, Tides and the shell accents were too bright to stand units on.
- **Not seen:** a long match with towns growing; the Low quality setting; how it sits with #812's age air at dusk.
