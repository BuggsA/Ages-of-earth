# The Atomic Age: a sixth epoch and the Missile Silo (#391 s1, #605)

The owner asked for 6 or 7 epochs (#391). Step 1 adds the sixth, the Atomic Age, past the Industrial, and its one building.

- **Rules** (`src/sim/silo.ts`, `SILO` in `data.ts`): a Missile Silo (M, military menu) `launch`es at any map point; `SILO.fuse` (45 s) later every rival building within `SILO.reach` (5 tiles) takes 1500 and every rival land unit 400. The clock is public. A silo that falls while it counts scrubs the launch. A launch costs 600 gold + 200 stone and empties the silo for 240 s. Peace, wrong age and an empty purse refuse it. Own people and everything outside the ring are spared.
- **Epoch plumbing:** `EPOCHS` has six rows, `TRUNK`/`AGES` an `atomic` age (tier 5), `CARRY`, `FISH_CARRY`, `EPOCH_START_RES`, the computer's `SHARE`/`ADVANCE_AT`, and the citizen's hp line gained a sixth entry. Units and buildings clamp to their Industrial look and stats (`unitStats` already clamped); only the silo is new art. Aeronauts and Lightning stay side roads and return to Industrial first.
- **Computer** (`siloRoad`, `fireSilo`, `fleeWarhead` in `ai.ts`, `AI_SILO`): a clever computer raises one silo, picks the rival spot whose ring catches the most (a Town Center counts 4, a building 1, a soldier 2; threshold 6), sets the fuel aside while it is the only thing missing, launches, and runs its people out of a rival warhead's ring. An Industrial computer bound for Aeronauts or Lightning holds off the Atomic climb up to 300 s (`AI_SILO.hold`), so those roads keep working.
- **Player side:** Launch button on a selected silo (M, then click), a ☢ chip with the seconds left for every warhead in the air, a red ring on the target and a toast to the targeted player, a blast and pall where it lands, three synthesized sounds (`siloLaunch`, `siloBlast`, `siloScrub`), a tip, a web-map node (row 6).

**Numbers added to `data.ts`:** `EPOCHS[5]` (1800 food, 1500 gold, 700 stone, 95 s), `BUILDINGS.silo` (300 wood, 400 stone, 700 gold, 3000 hp, 80 s), `SILO`, `AI_SILO`, `CARRY[5]`, `FISH_CARRY[5]`, `EPOCH_START_RES[5]`, citizen line to six entries.

**Test edits, with the reason** (the old specs said "five epochs"; the owner's note #391 is what changes that): `tests/ages.test.ts` (trunk tiers 0–5, the last trunk age is `atomic`), `tests/web-map.test.ts` (six trunk ages visible at the start), `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` (one line for the new `launch` command, as that test asks).

**Decision added for the player:** where and when to spend one ruinous warhead everyone can watch, and whether to scatter, guard the silo or raid theirs.

**Not done:** the fission/fusion fork and fallout (seed #533); a seventh epoch; a keepsake for the silo; Atomic-only units (soldiers stay Industrial); the campaign proof and the slow tier were not run. Not seen: the game in a browser.

## Step 2: fallout (same day)

The ring a warhead struck stays poisoned for `FALLOUT.time` (90 s): each second every land unit in it, anyone's, takes `FALLOUT.dps` (4). Buildings are spared. `s.fallout`, `falloutStep`/`inFallout` in `silo.ts`; the computer's `fleeWarhead` now also pulls its people out of fallout; a green haze in `view.ts`. Decision added: press in after a strike and pay in health, or wait out the haze. Numbers added: `FALLOUT`. Not done: wind drift, hardened units, the fission/fusion fork. Test: `tests/silo.test.ts` (fallout hurts both sides, spares buildings and the far side, clears). Not seen in a browser.

## Step 3: the fork, Fission or Fusion (same day)

Two side ages off the Atomic Age behind a silo (`clue: 'silo'`), each `shuns` the other, each with a road back (`ROADS`, `AGES.fission`, `AGES.fusion`, `FISSION`, `FUSION`). `rulesOf(p)` in `silo.ts` gives the fuse, refuel, fuel, warheads aloft, clean strikes and the leak. Fission: 25 s clock, 100 s refuel, each launch poisons 3 tiles (`Fallout.r`) round your own silo for 60 s. Fusion: 70 s clock, no refuel, up to 3 aloft, 450 gold + 120 stone a shot, no fallout from its strikes (`Launch.clean`); its road is 2400 food, 3000 gold, 1500 stone, 150 s. The computer takes a branch on a coin of its own (`AI_SILO.fork`, `.fusion`, `.forkAfter`), only once it holds half the road's price. Web map: a row of its own (7). Decision added: whether your silos are fast and leaky or slow, clean and many. Numbers added: `FISSION`, `FUSION`, the roads' costs, `AI_SILO` fields. Tests: `tests/silo.test.ts` (3 more). Not done: a deed for each road, wind, hardened units, a seventh epoch. Not seen in a browser.
