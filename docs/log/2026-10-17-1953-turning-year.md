# The turning year (#479, Goal 1 s3)

Chain 1017-1811, session 5. Stayed off #467's functions (s1, chain 1017-1808).

- **What:** `src/sim/year.ts` (new) and `YEAR` in `data.ts`. Herd Plains: home herds (`Animal.town`, marked at the start) don't drift; they trek out to grazing 20–26 tiles toward the middle each autumn and come home each spring with a calf for each survivor (up to 6 a herd). Shore: `catchOf` scales fishing ×1.3 in autumn, ×0.3 in winter. Deep Wood: 5 deer come in to a yard by every town each winter (open tile 7–12 out with ≥3 trees within 2) and leave in spring. Glade: nothing, so tests, campaigns and replays don't change.
- **Deer walk in straight lines** (`stepToward` stops at the first blocked tile), and the first try stuck most herds in woods. Herds on the move now get a breadth-first search at the season's turn (8 directions, corners only between open tiles: a diagonal that grazes a blocked tile stops a deer there on rounding), kept as waypoints in `Animal.way`.
- **Computer:** `readYear` (clever: follow herds out to 34 tiles in autumn/winter; +2 hunters in the Deep Wood's winter), `huntTheYear` (moves a forager, else a farmer, onto game when the year brings it in reach, since farmers otherwise never switch), `spared` (leaves 2 of each own home herd), Shore boats count a third as farmers with ice ahead.
- **Measured** (`scripts/opening.ts 3 hunt hunt 15 medium <homeland>`, hard computers, before → after):

  - Deep Wood: meat hunted in the winter (minutes 7½–10): before +0 for both; after +484 and +461.
  - Shore: P1's stock at minute 10 goes 1097 → 682 under the ice; P2's 392 → 797. Both still age up.
