# The deep mine m4: the wet deep, gems, floods and the pump (#937, umbrella #924)

- `data.ts`: `STRATA[3]` "The wet deep" (`fill: 120`; every entry gains `fill`), `SHAFT.seams.gems` (`deep: true`: no plain grade), `SHAFT` `inrush`, `drown`, `pumpAge`, `pump`, `mill`, `drain`, `gemGrade`, `aiPump`, `aiGems`; `LODES[].wet` and `.gems`; `GEMS` (the Market's gem line). All new numbers.
- `shafts.ts`: `boundLayer` generalises m3's lode-only seam to any seam held below (gems, a lode's ore): `seamOf` falls back while that layer is flooded or spent, `seamProblem` says why, `placeFor` gives no other place and skips flooded layers. Floods: `waterAt`, `floodedAt`, `fillOf`, `seepStep` (each tick: rise, or drain while `pumping`), `riskFlood` (a full basket rolls `inrush × water`), `floodOut` (drowns on `drown`, or drives miners up). Pump: `pumpProblem`, `pumpKind` (`mill` after the Age of Dykes), `pumpCost`, `raisePump`.
- Gems ride as gold with `carryRare: 'gems'` and pay into `Player.gems` (`units.ts` `deposit`); they don't spill into a cache. The `trade` command sells them at `GameState.market.gems` (absent at the start price; `driftMarket` climbs it back). New command `pump` (a line in `tests/command-ownership.test.ts`). New event `flood`; `trade` events carry `gold` for gems.
- State: `Building.water`, `Building.pump`, `Player.gems`, `market.gems`, all optional. No `v` bump: an older save's shafts are at most layer 2, dry and unpumped.
- Computer (`ai.ts` deep mine section): it raises a pump at a shaft in the wet deep, or at one about to sink into it; it never sinks or blasts into it without one. A clever computer with a Market sets a pumped shaft to gems while a lot fetches `SHAFT.aiGems` or more, back to gold below it, and sells gems in `trade`.
- Look: the cut-away (drips, rising water, a flooded band, cyan gem glints, the pump's pipe and engine or sails, the core tip reads the flood time), the pithead model (variant +128 steam, +256 mill: a Cornish engine house or a windmill pump, a trough of lifted water), the core column (a water ring as high as the ground is wet, gem flecks), the inrush sound and spray, the flood toast, the gems in the resource bar, the Market's Sell gems button and gem price, a tip when water rises in an unpumped shaft. The seam buttons are keyed in the order they show (Q W E R T Y).
- Tests: `tests/deep-mine-wet.test.ts` (15, about 8 s).
