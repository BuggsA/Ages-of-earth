# More wild beasts to tame (2026-10-28)

The owner asked for more animals to tame, and for them to spawn too, "appropriately, not overwhelmingly so".

**Decision for the player:** none: more of the same beasts. A Wild town has enough to tame; the tame, breed and fight rules are unchanged.

## What changed (numbers in `src/sim/data.ts`)

| Number | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TAME.callCap` (boars and wolves near a camp) | 2 | 3 |
| `TAME.callNear` | 10 | 12 |
| `TAME.mammothNear`, `TAME.mammothFrom` | n/a | 24, 12 |

- `callWild` (`src/sim/tame.ts`): the cap counts boars and wolves only. In autumn a Hunting Camp of a Wild people also draws one mammoth from 12 tiles out, unless a wild mammoth stands within 24 tiles of it.
- `callNear` grew with the cap so beasts that wander a little still count as near the camp.

## Tried and dropped

More beasts placed at the start of the map (`MAMMOTH.extra`, `BOAR.extra`, `WOLF.dens` raised by one or so). It broke five unrelated seeded tests (`ai-coil-layout`, `ai-merc`, `missile-flight`, `mycelium`, `sim`), because extra animals changed what stood near their towns. Tests are read-only, so the map numbers are back as they were. Follow-up: raise them once those tests set up their own animals.
