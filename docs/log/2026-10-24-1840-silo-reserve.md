# The silo held in Reserve (#781, s3 of #759)

Chain 1024-1610, session 7. Branch `claude/781-silo-reserve`.

**Built:** a `reserve` command (owner-checked) and `Building.reserve` (the tick a held silo stands ready). A held silo launches nothing, and a salvo neither fires from it nor counts it. At `INTERCEPT.fire` seconds left, a warhead no battery took draws one attempt from the nearest ready Reserve silo of a rival (`reserveFor` in `silo.ts`). The attempt spends one warhead from that people's store and its own warhead's fuel, and is stored in `Launch.icpt` with `silo: true`. The silo then re-arms over `INTERCEPT.arm` seconds. The checksum mixes `reserve` and the flag. The model shows a teal band, raised doors, and an amber or teal beacon. The HUD shows the status, an R toggle and a line in the launch warning; there is also a tip and a toast. The computer holds its last silo while a rival stands a silo and it stands two (or its store is full), and stands it down to strike or to fire a salvo.

**Numbers:** `INTERCEPT.arm = 30`, `INTERCEPT.think = 10` (new). No save `v` bump: only optional fields were added.

**Seen in a browser:** the models, the HUD status, the R button, and the silo firing at a rival warhead (its warhead store and fuel dropping). Not yet heard: the sounds are the battery's.
