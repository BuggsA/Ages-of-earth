# Title camera stays inside the map (#421, #267)

Lane look. On the title the low film camera sits behind the town it looks in on. When that town was near the map's edge the lens ended up out past it, and the bottom of the frame showed the bare underside of the world: a pale cut-off band under the land (seen on seed 3, a Mongol town). The cinematic branch of `CameraRig.update` now holds the lens 2 tiles inside the map's bounds. No rule changes.

Seen: headless Chromium 1280x800 at seeds 3, 5, 11 (the title picks its towns at random, so the same frame can't be re-shot): no edge band in any. Not seen: every town on every map; a real GPU.
