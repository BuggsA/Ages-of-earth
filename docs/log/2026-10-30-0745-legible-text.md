# Legible text at every screen size

The owner (chat, 2026-10-30): "make text legible and readable. Sometimes it doesn't fit or goes off the screen … if you just make it smaller it likely won't be legible."

Measured first: a Playwright pass at 1366×768, 1280×800, 1024×640, 844×390 and 390×844 listed every text element off the screen, clipped, or under 11px. Fixed in `src/ui/style.css` (plus a span or two in `index.html` and `hud.ts`) by **making room, not shrinking text** (nothing below 11px; body text 13px+):

- **Top bar** spilled off the right below ~1180px (Sound and Menu unreachable at 1024) and "Stone Age" broke onto two lines out of the 40px bar. Now it narrows in steps: boxes tighten; then the people's name becomes its crest and Rivals/Roads/Workers/Menu become icons (tooltips keep the words); the age's progress bar tucks under its name. Extra resource chips (rare ores, launch warnings) scroll inside the resource strip instead of pushing buttons off. On a phone the whole bar swipes, and its drop-down panels anchor to the screen.
- **Banner and notices** were centred with `left: 50%` and so wrapped at half the screen; they now size to their text up to the screen width, and the age banner sits beside the tip box instead of running under it.
- **Command keys** (Q, W, Del…) were 10px on the icon art; now 11px on a dark chip. HP text 11px.
- **Selection panel**: long names wrap instead of "…"; long descriptions scroll instead of being cut off.
- **Title screen** on a short screen: content cut at the top (centred overflow); now `safe center` scrolls. **Setup sheet**: the right column and the seed row ran past the screen edge; grids now use `minmax(0, 1fr)`.
- **Web of Ages**: the map was shrunk to 68%, so age names rendered at ~8px; the card widens to show it at full size.
- **Phone, upright**: the selection gets its own row above the minimap and commands.

Not seen: every age's longer names in the top bar (only Stone Age was shot), tidings and the end report at small sizes.
