# A trailer and a step-in for the title screen (2026-10-19, Claude Code)

Owner ask: the title screen looked bad ("a bunch of options thrown at you with the game playing in the background"); it should be exciting, like a trailer you can skip, and feel like stepping into the adventure.

**Built:** `src/ui/prologue.ts`, new markup in `index.html`, a rewritten `#title` block in `style.css`. Look only: no rules, data, sim or save change.
- A skippable trailer over the title's live world (letterbox, four beats, the wordmark lands with a flash), once a session; any key or click or the Skip button ends it; "Watch the intro" replays it.
- The menu is the wordmark, one big Play with a sheen, and the three roads with a line each; the match settings moved into a **Customize** panel (the same elements and ids, so nothing that reads them changed). A line shows what Play will start.
- Play pushes the camera in, fades to black, starts the match under the dark and opens on whom you lead and the age (`'epoch'` chime), then the dark lifts on a camera easing back out.
- Embers, film grain and a warm sun glow sit over the title; `prefers-reduced-motion` and automation get no trailer or step-in (`?intro=1` forces them), so `tests/slow/browser-smoke.test.ts` is unchanged and passes.
- The campaign card's stray vertical seam (a `::after` line on every `.title-card`) is gone.

**Seen:** screenshots at 1300x800, 1024x680 and 390x780 of the beats, the logo landing, the menu, Customize and the step-in. The trailer's timing was checked by seeking its animations to fixed times, not by watching it run, and the sound by no means.

**Not done / next:** the match clock runs under the dark card (about 2 s of game time before the player can see); a pause until it lifts would be fairer. Sound for the beats (a low swell, a hit on the wordmark) needs a gesture first, so the trailer is silent until Play. A different world behind the trailer than the random attract match (a hand-framed fly-over) would be better still.
