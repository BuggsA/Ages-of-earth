# Wonder guard, judged properly (#374)

`scripts/ai-ab.ts` takes a 7th argument of extra game setup: `'{"startEpoch":3,"rich":3000}'` starts both seats in epoch 3 with 3,000 more wood, stone and gold, so both begin a Wonder. It also prints `wonders` (which seats began one).

`npx tsx scripts/ai-ab.ts 100 24 25 '{"wonderGuard":false}' '{}' '{"startEpoch":3,"rich":3000}'` → 48 games, a Wonder begun by both seats in all 48; guarded Hard (new) 24–24 against unguarded (old), gathered ×0.991 (se 0.050). Every game ended with a winner.

**Reading:** the guard neither helps nor hurts in this setup. Both seats raise a Wonder at once and the race is decided by whose finishes first; the guard's army post and response never changed an outcome. I did not tune `AI_WONDER`: no evidence it loses. A setup where one seat is the aggressor (e.g. only the old seat attacking early) would be a sharper test; not done.
Unseen in a browser; sim only.
