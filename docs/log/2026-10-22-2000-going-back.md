# Going back as a plan (#580, #555 s10)

- **Built:** buildings raised only in some ages (Drain Rig, Blood Tower, Transfusion Station, Caravanserai) now sleep while their people stands outside those ages, and wake on return. Before, they kept working on any road (only War Camps burned). `asleep` and `leaving` in `src/sim/ages.ts`; `burns` on the War Camp.
- **The plan:** the step-back button lists kept (the age counts as walked), sleeps (technologies, buildings), lost (War Camps); every road out of an age lists what sleeps, wakes and is lost. A selected sleeping building says when it wakes; a rival's says "asleep".
- **The computer:** its levy skips a sleeping rig (`bleedTown`, one line of `ai.ts`).
- **A nerf, on purpose:** a people that left the Path of Blood or the Silk Road kept its towers, rigs and inns working. Now it keeps one of them only once s11's keepsakes land.
- **Unseen in a browser:** the tooltips and status lines (the browser smoke test passes).
