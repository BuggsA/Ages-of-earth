# Flocks, s2 of the Steppe (#308, umbrella #297)

Chain 1015-1948, session 5, branch `claude/308-steppe-flocks`, lane rules, hard tier.

- New `src/sim/flocks.ts`: sheep as owned animals (`Animal.owner`, `kind: 'sheep'`), kept by the nearest Town Center (standing or on a wagon) within `FLOCK.follow`; grazing crops `GameState.grazed` (a per-tile Int32Array made with the first sheep); milk, fat, lambs, hunger; rustling by a rival within `FLOCK.steal` of an unwatched sheep, which then follows the rustler (`lead`).
- `animals.ts`: sheep skip the wild loop (a callback from `game.ts` runs them); wolves hunt sheep within `FLOCK.wolf` × the season's boldness, unless Hounds and the camp are near; a slaughtered sheep leaves `FLOCK.meat` + 2 × fat (a lamb `lambMeat`).
- Orders and commands: `milk` (citizens, own grown sheep), `lamb` (a steppe Town Center); `hunt` refuses an ally's flock, and a rival's during the peace.
- New numbers: `FLOCK` (all), `SEASONS[].grass` (1.2, 1, 0.8, 0.4).
- Look and sound: fat-tailed sheep with a team-dye daub, lambs at two-thirds size, heads dipping to graze; `GrazeView` draws cropped tiles as worn stubble; minimap dots in the owner's colour; `bleat`/`lambBleat`.
- HUD: L on a steppe Town Center (count/cap, hungry count in the tip); "Milking the flock" order words; tips `flock` and `rustle`; toasts for lambs, wolves, rustling and a grazed-out camp.
- `tests/flocks.test.ts` (12); `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` gets the `milk` and `lamb` rows it asks every new command for.
- Browser (headless Chromium, `npx vite`): see the pull request.
