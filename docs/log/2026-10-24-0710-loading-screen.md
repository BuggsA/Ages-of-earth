# A loading screen with some life

The screen before the title was bare gold text on a flat navy. Now: the same gold-gradient wordmark as the title card, the tagline "From the first fire to the age of steam", five lamps (the epochs) lighting in turn, and a sweeping gold bar. Pure HTML/CSS (`index.html`, `.loading*` in `src/ui/style.css`); respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. No rules, numbers or saves changed.

Seen: one headless Chromium shot at 1280x800 (JS blocked so the screen holds). Not seen: phone width, a slow real load.
