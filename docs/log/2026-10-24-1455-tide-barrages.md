# Barrages, breaches and the low-water strike (#748, s6b of #701)

Chain 1024-1330, session 2 of 8, lane rules, area:tides (hard). Follows #739 (the Age of Tides, PR #747).

- **Ponds:** `pondsOf` in `src/sim/tides.ts` walks every body of water and tidal ground (four ways; causeways and finished buildings stop it; polder, channels and sluice floods are not water to it). A body touching no map edge, no larger than `BARRAGE.most` tiles and bordering a causeway is a pond: its tidal tiles are marked in `GameState.pond`. Recounted in `settle` (every turn of the tide), after `raiseCauseways` (with a `pond` event for each new pond) and after a breach. Pond tiles never bare. `tideTurns` bumps when the pond set moves, so the terrain repaints (green bed, `POND` in `terrain.ts`).
- **Mills:** `millShares` claims a mill's pond tiles first, then the rest, up to `TIDE.mill.most`; pays `gold × (n + (BARRAGE.mill − 1) × pond) / most`.
- **Who laid it:** `GameState.causewayBy` (owner + 1), set in `raiseCauseways`.
- **Breach:** `breachAt(s, x, y, r, by)`: every causeway tile of a rival of `by` with its middle within `r` goes back to `bedLevel` (from `tideBed`; 0 = open sea), is bared or covered at the level the sea stands, sweeps land units on tiles now water, drowns kegs there, and recounts ponds. Called from `powder.ts` `blow` (`KEG.reach`) and `silo.ts` `strike` (`SILO.reach`). Event `breached`; stat `breached`.
- **Kegs on causeways:** command `kegTile` and order `kegTile` (`units.ts` `kegTileStep`, laid within `KEG.lay` of the tile); `kegTileProblem`. The HUD's "Lay a keg" now takes a click on a rival causeway too (`main.ts` `kegAt`).
- **Drowned fuses:** a keg on a tile the tide covers is lost (`drownKegs`, keg event `what: 'drowned'`).
- **The computer:** `barrageWork` (mem `tide.plan`, `seen`, `raised`): `barragePlan` from each finished mill in turn until one has a line; lays it in one `wall` drag with `causeway: true` once the whole line is affordable; mends gaps once it stood. `sapKegs` sends a sapper to `rivalBarrage` (an unguarded rival causeway tile beside a pond) before anything but a dyke. `military`: `ebbWait` gates the wave and its reinforcements (`ebbTiming` at the slowest soldier's speed; wait counted only while the wave stands ready; at most `AI_EBB.hold`); `turnBack` in `tideWork` recalls those not over the far shore `AI_TIDES.wary` s before the flood reaches the crossing's level.
- **Look, sound, HUD:** pond bed tint; effects for `breached`, `pond`, drowned kegs; sounds `pondShut`, `breach`; toasts for a pond shut, a breach (either side) and a drowned keg; Tide Mill status counts pond tiles; tips `barrage`, `breachCauseway`; order label; dev hook `ages.barrage(0)`.
- **Save:** no `v` bump: `causewayBy` and `pond` are optional typed arrays, absent in older saves (no ponds; older causeways have no owner, so powder breaches them whoever's they were).
- **Numbers new in data.ts:** `BARRAGE = { mill: 2, most: 160 }`, `AI_BARRAGE = { reach: 9, line: 10, least: 6, cost: 1, crew: 3 }`, `AI_EBB = { across: 3, slack: 1.25, margin: 6, hold: 200 }`.
- **Tests:** `tests/barrages.test.ts` (7), `tests/ai-barrages.test.ts` (3); `tests/command-ownership.test.ts` gains its `kegTile` line (the test is built to list every command).
- **Seen in a browser:** headless Chromium on the dev server, `ages.tides(0)` then `ages.barrage(0)`: the pond held its water through low water, a breach let it bare at the next turn, no page errors. The causeway paving itself was hard to make out at that zoom. Sounds unheard.
