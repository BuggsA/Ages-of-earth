# Keepsakes (#581, #555 s11)

- **Built:** `Age.keepsake` on nine side ages (Alchemy, Silk, Iron and Blood, Total War, Hemocraft, Blood-rite, Pilgrims, Fairs, Lightning). Leaving the age marks one building of its kind (`Building.keep`: the chosen one, else the oldest) and it keeps the age's work on any road. The `keepsake` command (J) chooses it while in the age.
- **The age's work follows the kept one:** the old mill grinds one mill's worth (`grindersOf`), vigils, the feast day, the Blood-rite altar (drawing and mending), the coil (bolt and field, `inKeptCoil`). Age-only buildings simply stay awake.
- **Also:** `enterAge` now notes the age left as walked (a people that began in a side age, say in a campaign, kept no record of it).
- **The computer:** keeps the oldest by default, and keeps its saltpetre while its old mill grinds (`ai.ts`, the ore sale).
- **Gaps:** Iron, Sail, Merchants, Aeronauts, Reclaimers, Warband, Steppe, Horde, Sappers name none yet (`docs/age-roads.md`).
- **Unseen in a browser:** the J button, the keepsake status lines and toast, the age panel's line.
