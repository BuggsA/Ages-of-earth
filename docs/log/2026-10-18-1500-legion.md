# The legion (#501, the Romans)

- Romans keep `LEGION.citizens` (12, +3 an epoch) citizens: `citizenProblem` refuses a Town Center train past it (live and queued). Their foot soldiers (`legionary`) gather wood and stone at `LEGION.rate` (0.8) and raise/mend any building (`canRaise` takes the civ). No new command, no new state, so no save bump; rules change makes older Roman replays differ.
- A legionary at work keeps watch (`sentry` in units.ts): a foe within `LEGION.watch` x sight turns him to fight with `back` set to his work. Barracks rally flags on a tree/stone send new legionaries to work. HUD: build menu and workforce panel include them; tip `legion`; README line; `VERB_GAPS` lost romans; work-pose model `LEGION_MODEL` (tools on his back).
- Computer: `legionWork` (idle soldiers beyond a guard of `aiGuard` go to timber/stone, `aiStone` to stone), builds with them (`construct`, foundations), `View.hands` replaces citizen counts in growth gates, the barracks trains foot to `aiHands`+epoch*`aiHandsEpoch`, citizens' wood/stone share is cut to `aiCitizenWood`.
- A/B (`scripts/civ-ab.ts`, new): Romans on Hard against Greeks, Egyptians and Chinese on Hard, seeds 1-6 x 2 seats per opponent, 25 min medium, 36 matches each. Loop off (old play): won 12, lost 7, ahead 7, behind 10. Loop on (final): won 12, lost 7, ahead 6, behind 11. Parity with the old play (matches are noisy at this size); worst against the Egyptians and Chinese, strong against Greeks (6-2).
- Existing tests use a Roman computer as a neutral opponent, so the computer's legion play is confined: soldiers work only through epoch `LEGION.aiUntil` (2), idle ones beyond a guard of 4. Also: the Roman computer can dig bog iron with soldiers (`legionKind` has `R_BOG`), surveys from 14 hands, and soldiers never take a farm (a bug the A/B found: builders of a farm were set to farm it).
- Slow tests with Romans (ai-blood, ai-web, ai-winter, features, landmarks, lords, naval, relics, ruins, trade, wild) pass except `ruins` and `wild` "puts a mammoth out", which fail on main too (#525).
- Not seen in a browser.
