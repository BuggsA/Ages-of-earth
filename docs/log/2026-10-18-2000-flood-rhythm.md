# The Egyptian computer plays the flood's rhythm (#517)

Branch `claude/bold-pascal-kslsdh`. Lane: ai.

**Built:** a clever Egyptian computer keeps its wonder for the flood (the corvée builds twice as fast), and in the spring before it leans 40% more of its hands to food (`AI_FLOOD` in `data.ts`, `floodComing` / `floodBuildHeld` in `ai.ts`). Tests: `tests/ai-flood-rhythm.test.ts`.

**Tried and dropped:** two extra fields in spring, and holding the house line outside the flood. In an A/B (Egyptian vs Roman, both Hard, seeds 1–4, small, 20 min, epochs summed) the fields cost epochs (8 vs 11) and the house hold lost seed 4's match; without them the Egyptians sit at 11, the same as before.

**Not done:** the issue's goal of closing the gap toward 19 (that figure came from the #516 script, which I didn't have; my A/B is a different setup). The spring food lean is untested against a real flood season in a full match beyond that A/B, and nothing here was seen in a browser. Walls were left alone: they only appear as scenario deeds.
