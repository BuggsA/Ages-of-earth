# Mist breathes, and never hazes the ground you look at (look only)

The owner, playing: the fog felt perpetual (a screenshot of a whole map washed grey). Cause: a day's weather is fixed for the match, mist is 15 to 30% of matches on most maps, and a thick mist cut view reach to ~0.25 of a clear day, so the haze began before the screen's centre.

- `breathe` in `src/render/weather.ts`: a mist day thins to 0.3 of its strength half a period (8 min) in, and thickens again; the view redraws the sky at 0.05 steps (`view.ts`, `seasonDay`). Not on forced (`?weather=`) days or the title. Other weather is unchanged.
- Mist reach `0.55 - 0.3k` became `0.7 - 0.25k` (0.45 at full strength).
- Haze never starts nearer than 1.25x the camera distance (`fog.near`), whatever the weather.
- Tests: new `tests/mist-breath.test.ts` (3); existing weather tests untouched and pass. `npm run check`: all pass.
- Headless Chromium, `?seed=7&weather=mist`, zoomed out, before and after: before the whole land is grey; after the land around the town is clear and the haze greys the distance. Screenshots in the session only, not committed. Mist at its thinnest and the breathing over time were not watched in play.
- Decision added for the player: none (look).
