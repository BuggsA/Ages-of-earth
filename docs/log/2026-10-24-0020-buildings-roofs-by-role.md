# Buildings s1: roofs by role, team rim and pennant (#625, area:buildings of #617)

- Roofs now say what a building is for (Middle Ages on): war under cold slate, faith pale and gold, trade teal, stores and camps ochre. Houses and the Town Center keep the epoch's red. Stone Age keeps thatch.
- Every building but walls, gates, snares, saplings, farms, Town Centers and Wonders gets a team-coloured rim round its pad and a tall team pennant at its front corner.
- Seen in the `town` scene (before/after in `test-results/before|after`, not committed): types separate at a glance; the rim is still thin from above (the Caravanserai test caps a model at footprint + 0.1, so the rim can't stick out further).
- Not done: a big mass of team colour (roof ridge, awnings, door cloth) per type; distinct silhouettes for same-box buildings (house vs storehouse vs barracks at epoch 3); Stone Age and Bronze shots; the small prop views.
- Next: team colour as a large part of each roof or awning, then silhouettes.
