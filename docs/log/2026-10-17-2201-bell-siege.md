# The bell comes down on a parked siege (#487)

A clever computer's town bell used to wait for at most `bell.quiet` enemy soldiers within 12 tiles of any building. A band parked at an outlying house kept the whole town sheltered for the rest of the match. Now, once the bell has rung `bell.patience` (90 s), only foes within `bell.reach` (7 tiles) of a building count toward the all-clear (`townBell`, `threatNear(…, within)` in `src/sim/ai.ts`). Raiders at the walls still keep the citizens in. New numbers in `AI_LEVELS[*].bell` (`src/sim/data.ts`): `patience: 90`, `reach: 7`. Test: `tests/ai-beaten.test.ts` (fails on the old code). Not tried: the seed 3 Deep Wood repro from the issue, in a full match.
