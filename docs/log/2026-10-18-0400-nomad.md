# Born on the move (#501, the Mongols)

- `isMobile` (`src/sim/steppe.ts`) is true for a people with `CIVS[civ].nomad` in every age, so the Steppe's packing, pitching, lambs and breeding are the Mongols' from minute 1. `createGame` gifts `NOMAD.start` (4) sheep. New `src/sim/nomad.ts`: `isNomad`, `quarries` (no stone; joined into `mineable`), `yurtRoom` (a finished or packed House keeps `NOMAD.house` sheep in `flockCap`), `stoneTribute`.
- No Farm in any age (`buildBarred`). Nomad flock: milk ×`NOMAD.milk` (2) a bite, regrow ×`NOMAD.regrow` (0.25), grazing ring `NOMAD.range` (8; `grazeRange` in flocks.ts), milking speed × the `farm` mod (`milkRate`: Plow, Irrigation). Tribute (`tributeStep`): a nomad taker also takes `NOMAD.stone` (0.6) stone per gold due, even from an empty purse; event `tribute` gets `stone`, stat `tributeStone`. `CIVS.mongols.add.tribute` 0.15.
- Computer (`ai.ts`): stone share goes to gold, wood ×`aiWood`; lambs from food in hand while the flock is under `aiFlock`; a milker per `aiPerMilker` sheep; strikes camp every `aiEvery` s at most, searching to `aiFar` and `aiSideways`; Market from the Bronze Age at `aiMarket` citizens, buys stone and grain (any level); tribute band of `aiRiders` (4) off the Steppe Road, gathered at home unless a merchant rival's road calls (`watchPost`), standing outside its supply; packs only after the flock is hungry for `aiHungry` (30) s. Still rides for the Steppe Road from the Stone Age; on the Steppe Road itself it keeps the road's old play (the steppe and horde tests use a Mongol computer).
- HUD: stone and food tooltips, lamb button text; tips `nomad`, `nomadStone`; the `farm` tip skips them; tribute toast and grey stone chips with the gold. README line, `VERB_GAPS` lost mongols, `civ-ab.ts off` deletes `nomad`.
- No new command or state field beyond an optional stat and event field: no save bump. Older Mongol replays differ.
