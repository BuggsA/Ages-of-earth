**Built:** trading posts and traders. The map generator places `TRADE.posts` posts (2, 2, 3 by size) within `TRADE.edge` tiles of the edge, about as far from their two nearest towns (`fair`), at least `far` of the map's width from every town and `spacing` of it from each other, on level ground every town can reach. Looser passes widen the band, let trees stand in the way and, as a last resort, allow anywhere. The posts roll their own dice and only clear trees from their plaza, so relics and the rest of the map are unchanged. The Market trains traders (`UNITS.trader`, class `trader`); the `route` command and the `trade` order send them between their Market and a post, loading gold worth `tripGold` at the post and paying it in at home (`caravan` events). A fallen trader loses its load. A fallen Market sends them to the nearest other one; with none, they wait with the gold until a Market is right-clicked. A rally point on a post puts new traders on the road. Nothing can be built on a plaza (`postOnTile`). Two technologies at the Market: Caravans and Banking. The computer saves for a Market when posts exist, trains traders ahead of its epoch savings, picks the best-paying road that keeps clear of rival towns, and sends idle riders after rival traders near home; it no longer trains traders as soldiers or counts enemy traders as a threat. View: `src/render/postsView.ts` (the caravanserai), four trader models (`unitModels.ts`, with a homeward model carrying gold), coin bursts, sounds `tradeLoad` and `tradePaid`, the post panel, the trader's status and pay per trip, Market keys **T** and **R F**, minimap marks, a tip, the help text, a "Traded" column on the end screen.
