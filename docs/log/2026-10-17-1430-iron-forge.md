# Iron Age forge (#356 s4)

The audit found the Iron Age was numbers only (cheaper foot, no horse, bog iron pays double). Added the **forge**: every 50 bars of bog iron dug past the 150 that opened the road gives foot soldiers +1 attack, up to +3 (`IRON_FORGE` in `data.ts`, `ironSteps` in `stats.ts`; the stats cache is bumped as each step is dug). The marsh becomes an armoury to hold and to raid; the computer already digs, so it forges too.
