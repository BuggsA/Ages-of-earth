# Signature soldiers (Chain 1013-1900, session 1)

Pitch and build: every people gets a signature unit line (`SIGNATURES`, `src/sim/data.ts`), applied in `statsFor` (`src/sim/stats.ts`) so combat, HUD names and tooltips pick it up. Cost is deliberately untouched (commands and the computer price from the base table). Test: `tests/signatures.test.ts`. No save bump: no state shape change. Not tried in a browser.
Numbers: Egyptian priests range +1, train x0.8; Greek infantry parmor +1, speed x0.95; Roman infantry attack +1, armor +1, train x1.15; Mongol cavalry attack +2, hp x0.9; Chinese siege attack x1.15, reload x0.9; British ranged attack +1, sight +1, speed x0.95.
