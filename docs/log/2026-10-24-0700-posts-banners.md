# Posts out of the team hues, bigger landmark banners (#625, #617)

Trading posts used red, blue and gold, the players' colours, so a post could read as someone's building. Pavilions, rugs, pennants and wares are now ochre, olive, umber and cream. Landmark keeper banners are 2.0 m tall with a 0.55×0.8 cloth (were 1.4, 0.34×0.5), so who holds a spring or crag reads at the default zoom.

Render only; no `data.ts` numbers. Seen: `tests/models.test.ts` builds all models. Not seen in a browser (no shot scene for props).
