# The Water Road, s1: the Age of Dykes (#701)

Chain 1024-2000, session 1 of 8, lane rules (hard).

- **The water layer:** `GameState.polder` (per tile, 1 where water was pumped out), `src/sim/water.ts`. A drained tile becomes `T_GRASS`, loses `B_TERRAIN` and any shoal, and its corners rise to `POLDER.ground`; `blockVersion` bumps so routes and distance fields see the new land. Water flows four ways (a stepped dyke line holds). `checksum` folds in the polder tiles.
- **Dykes:** a 1-tile building on shallow water (`shallow`: every corner at `DYKE.depth` or higher) or on the shore beside it. `stampBuilding` treats a dyke as a gate along its whole length: its team walks the crown, rivals and ships don't pass; when it falls the water (`B_TERRAIN`) comes back. Placed by drag like walls (`wall` command with `dyke: true`). Walls' damage rules and "walls alone don't keep a people alive" include it.
- **Windmills:** `pumpStep` (every `POLDER.every` s, each mill on its own beat): the first body of water within `POLDER.reach` that is a basin (no map edge, at most `POLDER.most` tiles) drains one tile, nearest first, skipping one a boat is on. Asleep outside the Age of Dykes (`BUILDINGS.windmill.ages`).
- **Polder fields:** a Farm mostly on polder works `POLDER.farm` (1.5×) as fast (`polderField`, in `farmStep`).
- **The age and roads:** `AGES.dykes` (tier 2, verb/door/good, `routes: []`, no walls or gates); `middle → dykes` with a Dock as the clue and a new `coast` door (`coastHeld`: `DYKE.need` shallow tiles within `DYKE.home` of the start); roads back to the Middle Ages and on to Gunpowder.
- **The computer:** `dykePlan` finds the best straight dyke (or a lake) near home; `wants('dykes')` on a coin of its own (salt 0xd1e5), so no other match's dice move. `clueDue` raises the Dock, the Middle Ages hold up to `AI_DYKE.hold` s, `turnRoad` turns; `dykeWork` lays the line, raises mills (`millSpotFor`), then fields on the polder; it stays in the age until the basin is dry or `hold` passes.
- **Look and sound:** `TerrainView` repaints changed tiles (`sync`): drained ground rises out of the water over about a second, wet mud drying to striped field with ditches over 20 s; the water's bed texture follows (`refreshBed`) so the foam moves to the new shore; the minimap repaints polder. Dyke model (earth bank, stone face, turf, crown path joining neighbours), smock windmill with sails that turn fast while pumping (`windmillSails`, `SAIL_HUB`). Sound `pump`: a wooden creak and water rushing out.
- **The owner's reshaping (while this was built):** #701 is now a third main trunk of seven ages (Stone → Rivers → Dykes → Water Line → Locks → Tides → Floating Cities). This branch is the trunk's groundwork and its tier-2 age, reached for now by a turn off the Middle Ages (one of the crossings the trunk keeps). Its coast door is a stand-in until the Age of Rivers brings a stream to every start; the trunk must never be shut, so the Rivers step removes it.
