# The Tide Gate (#785, s6f of #701)

Chain 1024-1330, session 6 of 8, lane rules, area:tides (hard). Follows #779 (the Cockler's Hut, PR #784).

- **The gate:** `tidegate` in `BUILDINGS` (size 1, 600 hp, 80 wood + 120 stone, 20 s, epoch 5, `ages: ['tides']`, military key **U**). Placement: `tideGateSpot` (an own causeway tile, nothing on it, with a pond beside it), checked first in `placeProblem` (`'barrage'`). Stamped as a dyke (`stampBuilding`, `completeBuilding`): its own side walks over at every hour.
- **Numbers:** `TIDE_GATE = { least: 4, spread: 2, reach: 7, secs: 5, hurt: 40, wary: 8 }`, `AI_GATE = { every: 20, look: 1, threat: 3, shut: 8 }`.
- **Pond state:** `GameState.pondOut` (tiles let out) and `GameState.scour` (running scours). `recountPonds` → `letOut`: a let-out tile fills again when the sea covers it with no open gate on its pond; an open working gate lets its whole pond out; let-out tiles drop out of `s.pond`, so every reader of `isPond` (settle, mills, routes, beds, work) sees open flats. Both are in `checksum`.
- **The scour:** `toggleGate` (the `pack` command at a gate, refused while a scour runs): opens, and with a head ≥ `least`, covers `scourTiles` (a walk from the gate's sea side through water and bared flats, never the pond, causeways or buildings), sweeps the land units there with `sweep(s, u, TIDE_GATE.hurt, owner)` (event `tideSwept.by`, `stats.scoured`), drowns kegs. `settle` holds the scour's flats and its pond under (`scourHeld`); `endScours` in `tideStep` bares them after `secs`.
- **Risk:** `gateRisk(s, owner)`: a test over every loaded rival gate's `gateReach`, worked out once a tick (`reachMemo`, keyed by tick and `tideTurns`). Read by `passage`/`ebbFrom`/`tideRoute`/`rereadRoutes`, `tideWorkHold` (a picker waits ashore `wary` seconds), `fleeFlood`, and the computer's `cockleWork` bed search.
- **Breach:** `breachAt` wrecks a gate standing on a tile it breaches.
- **Computer:** `gateWork` in `tideWork`: sets a gate at `tideGateSpotIn` (its causeway tiles by a pond, the one nearest the middle with the sea on the far side); fires it on `threat` enemy land units on its reach (more than its own); shuts it `shut` seconds on.
- **Look and sound:** `tideGate` model (`tideModels.ts`: piers, lintel, capstan, mitred leaves shut in a V or swung back, gauge board, white water when open); variant = causeway arms + 16 open. `tideGate` event: white water, two rings and the `scour` cue (sound.ts), or a knock when shut. Toasts for your scour and for a rival's sweeping your people. HUD panel line (public) and the O button. Tip `tideGate`. Dev hook `ages.tideGate(owner, foes)`.
- **Tests:** `tests/tide-gate.test.ts` (10): placement, holding, the scour, refill after shutting, no scour at high water, rival risk (routes and idle workers), breach wrecks it, save/load mid-scour, the computer sets one and fires it.
- **Save:** no `v` bump: two optional fields.
