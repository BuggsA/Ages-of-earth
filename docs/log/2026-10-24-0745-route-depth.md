# Route depth: why a bound computer stopped short (#555 s0 follow-up)

`routes.ts` now takes `--start 2` (both seats start in the Middle Ages, so the late route ages are tested directly) and `--detail` (each match's climb with minutes, and the route ages the seat gave up). `playRoute` records `reached` and `gaveUp`.

## Found, per route (4 seeds, 40 min, Hard bound vs Normal free)

- **Army, the Warband never taken (seed 1):** the Barracks, the road's clue, was never built. The town's usual order lost the wood to cheaper buildings (the climb's savings kept the budget under water), and for a Greek city (`polis`) the AI skipped the Barracks outright because its line, foot, is barred. `worthWaiting` was never asked: the road was unseen. **Fixed:** `clueDue` in `ai.ts`, a clue of a road it has chosen is raised first, paid from what the town holds, and set aside until it is (not for the steppe, whose camp has its own order).
- **Army, Iron and Blood always given up** (every Middle-start seed, `gave up ironblood in warband@16–21`): the 600-gold plunder deed is a quarter of each kill's price; the bound computer slays 40–50 units in the age and banks about 160 gold. 600 asks for some 170 kills inside `AI_BLOOD.patience` (480 s). **Not fixed:** patience cannot make up the gap; the deed's number or the AI's raiding (buildings and citizens, not soldiers) must change. A player's raids may do better; the owner's play should say.
- **Faith:** takes Pilgrims in every Middle-start seed and a second step in seed 1 from Bronze; nothing to fix.
- **Economy:** the Merchants' age and Fairs are taken in 2 of 4 Bronze-start seeds; from the Middle Ages the Merchants (a Bronze turn) are out of reach by design, so it reaches Lightning only.
- **Horse:** from the Middle Ages the Horde needs the steppe walked first, so the route is empty there (0/4): a Middle start tests nothing for Horse.
- **Navy:** the Age of Sail is taken in 4/4 on `sea`, 1/4 on `lakes` from the Middle Ages (the catch of 500 fish on a lake).
