# The Sappers get a building of their own: the Mine Gallery (#727)

Chain 1025-1752, session 2. Merchants' Hire Hall (#808) is still open and untouched; this takes the Age of Sappers instead.

The **Mine Gallery** (`gallery`, key P, Sappers only, 100 wood 80 stone): a keg laid within `GALLERY.mine` (6) tiles of your own gallery is mined (`Keg.mined`) and a rival on foot can't stamp it out; a gallery also listens, countermining any rival keg laid within `GALLERY.listen` (6) tiles of it (it hears even mined kegs). It is the age's keepsake: kept (J), it listens on any road. The computer raises one 14 tiles toward the map centre once it holds a keg and a Barracks (`raiseGallery`); it does not yet aim its keg runs at targets near it.

**Decision:** dig in near the rival (exposed, but your kegs can't be doused) or lay kegs in the open and risk a stamp-out; and a rival who sees a gallery must raze it or keep its own kegs away.

**Verbs (docs/verbs.md):** verb: lay powder where nothing can reach the fuse; door: a building to raise and defend near the enemy; two players: a forward mine vs. kegs under army cover; answer: raze it, or hold a gallery of your own to listen.

New model (`gallery` in `buildingModels.ts`: spoil heap ring, shored shaft mouth, windlass, spoil cart, keg), not a recolour. Stockade's button glyph still undrawn.
