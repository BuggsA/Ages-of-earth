# Salvage: the Age of Reclaimers strips the rubble of fallen buildings (#503)

**Decision it gives the player:** when a building falls (a rival's on the field, or your own), strip its rubble into stock, or, for your own, leave the foundations to raise again at 40%. Stripping a rival's rubble sends citizens to a battlefield; stripping your own gives up the cheap rebuild.

**Verbs rubric (`docs/verbs.md`):** (1) verb: citizens right-click rubble to strip half a fallen building's price into stock; war pays the economy. (2) door: soldiers still train 25% slower, and stripping your own rubble spends its foundations. (3) two players: rebuild fast vs. cash the stones; raid and salvage vs. turtle and rebuild. (4) answer: rubble is a visible heap, so a rival can guard or contest the ground, and sees you came for it.
