# Route sweep: do the basic routes win? (#555 s0, lane ai)

Built `scripts/route-ab.ts`: one Hard computer is told to take a route's side ages, the other keeps to the plain trunk; each seed is played twice, seats swapped. Usage: `npx tsx scripts/route-ab.ts <army|navy|economy|faith|horde> <first seed> <seeds> <minutes>`.

First run, 12 seeds (24 matches) a route, 30 minutes, seeds 101-112, medium maps:

| Route | Route seat won–lost | Undecided (ahead/behind) | Gathered vs trunk | Walked the route's last age |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| army | 9–10 | 2/3 | ×1.01 | Total War in 0 of 24 |
| navy | 9–10 | 3/2 | ×1.01 | Aeronauts in 3 of 24 |
| economy | 6–10 | 5/3 | ×0.89 | Lightning in 1 of 24 |
| faith | 10–9 | 3/2 | ×1.02 | Fairs in 3 of 24 |

Read: no route is clearly better or worse than the trunk at this sample size (24 matches swing several wins on noise; economy's 6–10 is the one to recheck with 48). The routes' last ages are rarely reached inside 30 minutes, so the sweep mostly judges the early side ages. Next: rerun at 45 minutes, and see why the navy seat sometimes stays in the Stone Age (seeds 103, 104 seat 1: "Stone" only, a lost match) - that looks like an opening problem on certain seeds, not a route one.
