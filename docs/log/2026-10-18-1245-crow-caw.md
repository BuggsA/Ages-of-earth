# Crows caw (look)

**Decision it gives the player:** none: look/sound/fix. A caw from over a spoiling carcass tells you by ear what the crows tell the eye.

- `sound.ts`: new `caw` (two or three falling sawtooth barks with a bandpass rasp, synthesized).
- `crows.ts`: `nearest` exposes the closest crowed carcass; `view.ts` cues a caw every 7–19 s at it, panned and faded by the usual `cue` distance rules. README line extended.
- Not tried: a browser, or the level against other ambience; `npm run check` passes (824 tests).
